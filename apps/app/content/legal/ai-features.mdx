<Callout title="Summary">

- Every AI request goes through Vercel AI Gateway to the provider of the model for that feature: Anthropic's Claude for the AI theme chat and prompt enhancement, Google's Gemini for designing the theme, and OpenAI's GPT Image 2 for AI QR art.
- AI themes send your prompt, the chat so far and any images you attach. AI QR art sends the code's content (as a plain QR image), your prompt and any reference image.
- QRdx keeps a usage record (which model, how much, when) to apply your plan's limits. We don't store your prompts or images on our servers, and we don't train any models.
- Vercel says the gateway keeps no prompts or outputs once a request is done. We send requests only to providers that, under Vercel's agreements, don't train on prompts. Details below.
- AI output can be wrong or hard to scan. Check it before you print it. How you use it is up to you.

</Callout>

## Which features use AI

| Feature | Provider | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI themes** (the AI chat in the editor) | Anthropic (Claude) for the chat, then Google (Gemini) to design the theme, both through Vercel AI Gateway | Your prompt, the earlier messages in that chat, images you attach (JPEG, PNG, WebP or SVG) and the theme settings the model works with. Both models receive them |
| **Prompt enhancement** | Anthropic (Claude), through Vercel AI Gateway | The prompt you ask it to improve, with any images and themes you mention in it |
| **AI QR art** | OpenAI (GPT Image 2), through Vercel AI Gateway | The text or link your code contains, rendered as a plain QR image, your prompt, the things to avoid, the style settings, and a reference image if you add one |

You need to be signed in to use these features. Don't put passwords, personal data about other people or confidential information into prompts or images.

## What QRdx keeps

- **On our servers:** a usage record for each request: your account, the model, the tokens or credits used and the date. We use it to apply your plan's limits and to watch costs. It's deleted with your account.
- **In your browser:** your AI theme chat history, unsent drafts and recent AI themes, so you can pick up where you left off. The [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy) lists these items. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
- **Not kept:** we don't store your prompts, attached images or AI art results on our servers. AI art results are sent straight back to your browser. Download the ones you want to keep.

## What providers may do with it

**Vercel (AI Gateway).** Every AI request goes to Vercel's AI Gateway, which sends it on to the model's provider and bills QRdx for the usage. Vercel's documentation says the gateway doesn't keep prompts or outputs once a request is complete and doesn't use them for training ([zero data retention](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/security-and-compliance/zdr), [prompt training](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/security-and-compliance/disallow-prompt-training)). Vercel's [AI Product Terms](https://vercel.com/legal/ai-product-terms) apply to our use of the gateway.

**Anthropic, Google and OpenAI.** The gateway sends each request to one provider: Anthropic for the AI theme chat and prompt enhancement, Google, through its Gemini API or Vertex AI, for theme design, and OpenAI for AI QR art. QRdx limits each request to those providers, so the gateway doesn't pass it to another cloud. Under Vercel's AI Product Terms, content a provider receives through the gateway is subject to that provider's terms. Vercel [lists](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/security-and-compliance/disallow-prompt-training) Anthropic, Google and OpenAI among the providers that, under its agreements, don't use prompts for training, and QRdx asks the gateway to route only to such providers, so a request fails rather than reach one that might. We don't turn on the gateway's zero data retention option, so a provider may keep request data for as long as its terms allow.

**OpenAI, for AI QR art.** OpenAI's [API documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) says data sent to its API isn't used to train its models unless the customer opts in, and that it keeps abuse monitoring logs, image generation included, for up to 30 days. It also scans uploaded images for child sexual abuse material and keeps a flagged image for manual review. Vercel's [model page](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway/models/gpt-image-2) says zero data retention isn't available for GPT Image 2 on the gateway.

Vercel, Anthropic, Google and OpenAI are on our [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) list.

## Your responsibility for the results

- **Check before you use it.** AI output can be inaccurate, unexpected, or similar to other people's work. QRdx checks that every AI art image decodes before you get it, but it can still be harder to scan than a plain code: test it at the size you'll print, for example with the [scan checker](/tools/scan-checker).
- **Your inputs.** Only upload images and write prompts you have the right to use.
- **Your outputs.** As between you and QRdx, you own what you generate, to the extent the law allows. We can't promise that an output is unique or that it can be protected as intellectual property.
- **Follow the rules.** Don't use AI features to make anything the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use-policy) bans, realistic fakes of real people, or other people's logos and trademarks without permission.

## Limits and credits

AI themes and AI art have usage limits by plan. AI art uses one credit per delivered image, credits reset on the first of each month (UTC), and images that fail or don't pass the scan check use no credit. Current numbers are in [Plans and limits](/docs/plans-and-limits) and the [AI QR art](/docs/features/art-qr) guide.

## Self-hosting

If you run your own copy of QRdx, you choose whether to turn on AI features. They run through your own Vercel AI Gateway key, so the terms you agreed with Vercel, and the models you configure, apply.
