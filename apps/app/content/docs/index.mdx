QRdx is an open-source (AGPL-3.0) QR code platform. Design static QR codes for free with no account, or sign in for dynamic codes you can change after printing, mobile pages and scan analytics.

Every dynamic code keeps redirecting on every plan, even after a downgrade or cancellation. That's the [Never-Hostage Guarantee](/guarantee).
