---
title: Delete your account
description: Permanently delete your QRdx account and what's in it. Your dynamic codes stop working.
---

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting your account **can't be undone**. The dynamic codes in your own workspace stop working: scanners see "QR code not found". [Export your data](/docs/help/account-and-billing/export-your-data) first.
</Callout>

## Delete

1. Go to **Settings** → **Profile & security**.
2. Under **Danger zone**, click **Delete account…**.
3. Type `delete` to confirm.
4. Show it's you. What we ask for depends on whether you've turned on [two-step verification](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification):
   - **It's on:** enter a code from your authenticator app, or one of your backup codes. We don't email you a code. Each backup code works only once.
   - **It's off:** click **Email me a code** and type the 6-digit code we send you.
5. Click **Delete account**.

If two-step verification is on and you've lost both your phone and your backup codes, see [Lost your phone and your backup codes](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification#lost-your-phone-and-your-backup-codes).

## What gets deleted

Your own workspace: its codes, scans, pages, files and domains, plus your themes, webhooks and integrations. You're signed out on every device.

If you invited other people into your own workspace, it isn't deleted: like any shared workspace (below), another member becomes owner and keeps it.

## Your subscription

If you pay for Pro or Business, QRdx cancels the subscription straight away.

## Your other workspaces

- In a workspace where you're the only member, the workspace is deleted.
- In a workspace with other members, another member becomes owner and keeps the codes and pages you created there.

## Just want to stop paying?

You don't need to delete your account. [Cancel your subscription](/docs/help/account-and-billing/cancel) and your codes keep working on Free.
