<IfAvailable feature="invoiceRequests" title="Pay by invoice">

Checkout takes cards and wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay. If your company pays by bank transfer or needs an invoice first, you can ask for one for a year of Business.

1. Sign in and open the [pricing page](/pricing).
2. Choose **Yearly** and click **Pay by invoice instead** under the Business plan.
3. Enter your company name, the billing email address and country, and if you need them, your tax or VAT ID, billing address and a purchase order number.
4. Click **Send request**.

The request reaches the QRdx team by email. We arrange invoices by hand and reply to the billing email with the invoice and how to pay it. Nothing is charged when you send the request.

If the form can't send your request, email [enterprise@qrdx.dev](mailto:enterprise@qrdx.dev) with the same details.

</IfAvailable>
