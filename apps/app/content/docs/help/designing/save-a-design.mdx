---
title: Save and reuse a design
description: Save a design as a theme, reuse it on new codes and share the style with others.
---

A **theme** is a saved design: colors, dots, eyes, logo and frame. It doesn't include your link or other content.

## Save a design as a theme

1. In the [playground](/playground), click the heart icon above the preview, or press <kbd>S</kbd>.
2. Sign in if you're asked to.
3. Name the theme and click **Save Theme**.

Your themes are on the **Themes** page in your dashboard. The Free plan saves up to 10 themes; Pro and Business have no limit.

## Reuse a theme

On the **Themes** page, open a theme's menu:

- **Edit in playground** opens it in the editor.
- **Set as default** uses it for your new codes.
- **Copy to this workspace** shares a copy with everyone in the workspace you have open. Owners and admins see it in workspaces other than their own.
- **Rename**, **Copy link**, **Publish** and **Delete** do what they say.

**New theme** at the top of the page starts a new one.

## Share a style

Click the share icon above the preview, or press <kbd>X</kbd>. QRdx asks you to save the design first, then gives you a link.

The link shares the style, not your content. Anyone who opens it gets your colors, dots and eyes in the playground and adds their own link.

## Publish to the community

After you save a design, you can publish it to the [community](/community) for others to reuse. A custom frame can be published on its own from the editor's Frame section (**Publish frame**).

- It must scan Great (85 or more) with a link and with Wi-Fi details. QRdx checks when you publish.
- Uploaded logos and image art are never published.
- Everything in the community is free under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): anyone can use it for anything, with no credit needed.
- Publishing makes a snapshot: later edits to your saved design don't change what you published.
