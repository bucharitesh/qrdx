## Logo QR and Image QR

In the [playground](/playground?tab=art), open the **Art** tab. Choose a mode:

- **Logo QR** colors the dots inside your logo's shape.
- **Image QR** turns the whole code into your photo.
- **Off** turns art off.

Upload your own picture, or try a sample. Logos work best as a transparent PNG or an SVG. Photos work best with one clear subject.

Art works in every download format, and there's no AI involved.

## Balance art and scanning

Choose how strong the art is: **Subtle**, **Balanced** or **Bold**. Stronger art looks better but is harder to scan.

The **Scan check** below the art decodes your real code in your browser at three sizes. If it isn't great, it lists what to fix.

For the best results, set **Error correction** to **High** and keep a clear border around the code.

With the Logo QR badge, the logo only grows as far as the code can repair. A short link makes a small code, so the logo stays small, and the smallest codes show no logo at all. Error correction **High** gives it the most room.

<IfAvailable feature="aiArt" title="AI art">

[AI QR art](/ai/art) generates artwork around a code that still scans. It needs a free account.

In the playground, pick **AI art** in the **Art** tab. It paints the code you're editing. If that code is linked to a dynamic code, the art encodes the short link, so you can change where it points after printing. AI art is an image: download it as a PNG, or use **Use as Image QR** to turn it into a vector code for SVG, PDF or EPS.

Each plan includes a number of AI art images a month: 3 on Free, 30 on Pro and 150 on Business, shared by everyone in the workspace.
