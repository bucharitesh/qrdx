---
title: Team roles
description: What owners, admins and members can do in a workspace.
---

Every member of a workspace has one role. In your own workspace, you're the owner.

## Member

- Create and edit the workspace's codes and pages, and upload files.
- Delete their own codes, files and form responses.
- Use the workspace's shared themes and verified domains.

## Admin

Everything a member can do, plus:

- Delete any code, file or form response.
- Invite and remove members, and change their roles.
- Share themes with the workspace.
- Rename the workspace and change its URL.
- [Require two-step verification](/docs/help/teams/require-two-step-verification) for everyone in it (Business).
- Add, verify and remove custom domains.
- Manage webhooks.

## Owner

Everything an admin can do, plus:

- Make other members owners.
- Create and change OAuth apps.
- Delete the workspace (never someone's own workspace).

## Change someone's role

On the **Settings** → **Team** page, change the role next to their name. Only admins and owners can change roles.

Changing someone's role, removing someone, inviting an admin and deleting the workspace ask you to [confirm it's you](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification#confirm-its-you) first, unless you did in that browser in the last 10 minutes.

## Hand over a workspace

There's no separate transfer button. Make the new person an **Owner**. Once there's another owner, you can leave the workspace. Your own workspace can't be handed over this way: you can't leave it.
