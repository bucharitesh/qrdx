Scanova and QRdx both make dynamic QR codes with unlimited scans, hosted pages and analytics. Scanova sells plans priced by the number of dynamic codes, after a 14-day trial. QRdx has a permanent free plan and monthly or yearly paid plans with unlimited codes.

## Who each tool is for

Scanova suits organizations that want a vendor with an Enterprise tier. Basic is $5 a month, billed yearly only ($60 a year), for 3 dynamic codes. The other plans can be paid yearly or monthly: Lite is $108 a year or $15 a month for 10 codes, Standard $420 a year or $50 a month for 50, and Pro $900 a year or $100 a month for 500, with 5 users and 1 custom domain included on Pro. Ultra Lite is a one-time $150 for a single dynamic code with "lifetime validity".

QRdx suits individuals, small businesses and teams that want to start free and grow without per-code pricing. Free includes 10 dynamic codes and 3 hosted pages; Pro ($5/mo or $48/yr) makes both unlimited; Business ($15/mo or $144/yr) adds unlimited members, webhooks, conversion tracking and white-label pages.

## Where Scanova is the better choice

- **Enterprise procurement.** If your organisation wants a custom quote with limits sized to it, Scanova's Enterprise tier offers one. QRdx also signs [Enterprise](/enterprise) contracts, but they're its Business plan with written reply times and invoicing, so the limits stay Business's.

## Key differences

- **Trial versus free plan.** Scanova's pricing page states that a dynamic QR code will stop working once the free trial ends. QRdx has no trial, and under the [Never-Hostage Guarantee](/guarantee) dynamic codes keep redirecting on every plan, including after a downgrade or cancellation.
- **Billing.** Scanova's Basic plan is billed yearly only; Lite, Standard and Pro can be paid monthly or yearly. QRdx bills monthly or yearly on every paid plan.
- **Per-code pricing.** Scanova's plans are defined by dynamic code count (3 to 500). QRdx Pro and Business have no code limit.
- **Extra seats and domains.** Scanova charges $60 per extra user and $120 per extra domain a year on Pro. QRdx Business includes unlimited members and 25 domains.
- **Openness.** QRdx is AGPL-3.0 open source and can be self-hosted. Scanova is closed source.

## Switching from Scanova

1. Find out which Scanova codes are in print and whether they stay active on your current plan; they redirect through Scanova. [Do QR codes expire?](/blog/do-qr-codes-expire#first-find-out-which-kind-you-have) shows how to tell what a printed code holds.
2. Create QRdx codes for new print runs. Short links on Scanova's own domain can't be moved to QRdx.<IfAvailable feature="customDomains"> Codes on a custom domain you own can stay in print: import them, then [point that domain at QRdx](/docs/migrate#your-own-domain-go-yourbrand-com).</IfAvailable>
3. Rebuild hosted pages in the QRdx page editor and test each code on real phones before printing.
