## Confirm it's you without your mailbox

Without two-step verification, **Confirm it's you** used to offer only a code by email, so losing your mailbox meant you couldn't change your email address. Now you can also:

- use your passkey, if you have one;
- sign in again with a Google or GitHub account you've linked, or your workspace's single sign-on when it uses OIDC. You pick your account there, come back to the same page and make your change again.

Signing in again only counts as the account you're changing. Picking a different Google account doesn't switch you to another QRdx account or create a new one. [How it works](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification#confirm-its-you).

With two-step verification on, nothing changes: use your passkey, an authenticator code or a backup code.

## Lost access to your email

If you can't confirm it's you any other way, the help center now says what support needs before moving your account to a new address, and how long it takes. [Read it](/docs/help/account-and-billing/sign-in#lost-access-to-your-email).

## Changing your email signs out other devices

Once your email changes, QRdx signs you out on every other device and browser. The one you used stays signed in.

## Fixes

- The help pages said only your first passkey turns two-step verification on. Adding any passkey while it's off turns it on.
- The email change help described the old rule (a sign-in in the last 24 hours). It now asks you to confirm it's you, like other security changes.
