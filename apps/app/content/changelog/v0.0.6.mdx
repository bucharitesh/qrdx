## Faster, easier to find

- Pages render on the server again, so they show content sooner and search engines can read them.
- Links to QRdx, blog posts and docs pages now unfurl with a preview image in chats and social posts.
- The blog has an RSS feed, new illustrations, a table of contents on phones and a new guide, [Understanding QR codes](/blog/understanding-qr-codes), with an interactive error-correction demo.
- New [comparison pages](/compare) put QRdx next to other generators and libraries, with sourced and dated facts.

## Security

- Stricter checks on AI image URLs, rate limits on AI and sign-in routes, and a free AI quota that can't be overspent.
- Integration tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and integration sign-ins verify their state.

## qrdx library and CLI

- `qrdx` no longer adds a logo by default, respects the `size` prop, works in React Server Components and lists React as a peer dependency. PDF export's jsPDF is now optional.
- Fixes for QR detection, contrast checks and eye patterns.
- `@qrdx/cli` draws logos into PNG output, validates its flags and installs without sharp when your platform doesn't support it (SVG still works).

## Run it yourself

QRdx now ships a Dockerfile and a Compose file, with a [self-hosting guide](/docs/self-hosting). It boots with three environment variables and one sign-in provider; optional services switch off cleanly when they aren't configured.
