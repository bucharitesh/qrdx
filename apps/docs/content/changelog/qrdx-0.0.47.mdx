## New styles

- **14 body patterns**, including `liquid`, `classy`, `mosaic`, `random-dots`, `heart` and `star`. `liquid` and `classy` join neighbouring modules into one smooth shape.
- **9 eye frames** and **13 eye centres**, such as `leaf`, `drop`, `squircle`, `shield` and `flower`. `leaf`, `drop` and `petal` eyes turn to face their corner of the code.
- **20 call-to-action frames** with the `frame` option: banners, speech bubbles, a polaroid, a phone, a ticket, a menu card and more. Set the label, a second label and the label size.
- See every option drawn live at [qrdx.dev/docs/styles](/docs/styles).

## Colors and fit

- **Per-eye colors**: `eyeColors` gives each corner eye its own outer and inner color or gradient.
- **Module scale**: `moduleScale` (0.5 to 1) shrinks the modules for a spaced-out look. Each pattern stops at the smallest size that still scans.
- **Auto error level**: `autoLevel` raises the level to H when a logo covers more than 7% of the code.
- **Logo padding**: `imageSettings.padding` adds clear space around a logo.
- `bgColor="transparent"` leaves the background empty in PNG, SVG, PDF and EPS.

## Logo QR and Image QR

The `art` option blends a logo or photo into a real, scannable code. It is vector output with no AI. See [Art QR](/docs/features/art-qr).

- **Logo QR** has two looks: `blend` traces the logo into crisp shapes under a fine dot screen, and `badge` puts the logo on a plate in the middle of a code tinted with its colors.
- **Image QR** shows a photo in full color (`color`) or as a black-and-white halftone (`mono`).
- `matchEyes` colors the corner eyes from the logo.

## Print and export

- EPS export for print shops and design tools.
- Print-ready PDF, SVG and EPS at an exact physical size, with bleed, crop marks and CMYK colors.

## Will it scan?

- `getScanScore` estimates how reliably a design scans, and `getImageScanScore` checks an image you already have.
- `getStylePairRisk` warns about eye and pattern pairs that some scanners struggle with.

## Retired styles

`curly` eyes and `diamond`, `star` and `banner` eye centres are no longer offered, because some phone scanners can't read them. Saved designs that use them still render, but the CLI no longer accepts them.

## Upgrading from 0.0.46

- `QRCode` no longer adds a logo unless you pass `logo`.
- `react` and `react-dom` are now peer dependencies. PDF export needs `jspdf`, which you install yourself, and `qrdx` no longer installs sharp.
- `QRCodeSVG` and `QRCode` work in React Server Components.
- The `qrdx` command moved to its own package: run `npx @qrdx/cli generate`.

## @qrdx/cli 0.0.5

- Every new pattern, eye, eye centre and frame. Add a frame with `--frame`, `--frame-text`, `--frame-color` and `--frame-text-color`.
- EPS output: `-o qr.eps`, or `--format eps`.
- Logos are drawn into PNG output.
- Clear errors for bad flag values, listing the accepted ones.
- It installs without sharp where your platform doesn't support it. SVG and EPS still work.
