## Google Drive

- **Pick a file or folder** — connect Google Drive in the editor, browse your files and turn a file's or folder's share link into a QR code.
- **No connection needed for links** — paste a Google Drive, Docs, Sheets or Slides link and QRdx encodes it as it is.

## Integration pages

Each integration now has its own page that shows what it can do and what it needs, from creating short links to picking files, so you know before you connect an account.
