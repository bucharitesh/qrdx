## Community themes

- **Preview before you use** — open any theme in a larger preview, step to the next or previous one, and share a link to it.
- **Popular this week** — sort by the most liked themes this week, this month or of all time, or by newest.
- **Simpler filtering** — pick one tag at a time to narrow the list.
- Like counts load faster and stay in sync between the list and the preview.

## Dub short links

Connect your [Dub](https://dub.co) account in the editor to create a new short link or pick an existing one, and use it as the content of your QR code. Already have a Dub link? Paste it in without connecting anything.
