## For workspaces on Business

**Require two-step verification.** Owners and admins can turn it on in **Settings** → **Team**. Members without it see what to do when they open the workspace, and their other workspaces work as before. The members list shows who has it on. [How it works](/docs/help/teams/require-two-step-verification).

**Require single sign-on for your domain.** Once your domain is verified, turn on **Require single sign-on** on the **Single sign-on** page. Email codes, Google, GitHub and passkey sign-ins stop working for addresses at your domain, and their sessions last 24 hours, so someone you remove in your identity provider is out within a day. Owners keep their other ways in, so a problem with the identity provider can't lock everyone out. [Read more](/docs/help/teams/single-sign-on#require-single-sign-on).

**Open QRdx from your identity provider.** SAML connections now accept sign-ins started from your identity provider's app dashboard, and land on the page you set as the app's RelayState. Each response works once, and two-step verification still applies. [Set it up](/docs/help/teams/single-sign-on#open-qrdx-from-your-identity-provider).

Typing a work email on the sign-in page now offers **Continue with SSO** when your company signs in that way, before any code is emailed.

## Harder to use a stolen sign-in

- **A sign-in used somewhere new asks first.** If a sign-in is suddenly used from a different country *and* a different browser than where it started, QRdx asks you to confirm it's you before anything else. [Details](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/account-security#a-sign-in-used-somewhere-new).
- **Sign-ins last up to 30 days.** After that, QRdx asks you to sign in again, however much you use it. [More](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/account-security#how-long-you-stay-signed-in).
- **More changes ask you to confirm it's you:**
  - linking or unlinking Google or GitHub;
  - creating an API key, an OAuth app or a new client secret;
  - approving an app that asks to use your account;
  - adding a webhook or changing its URL, and showing or rotating its signing secret;
  - adding a Slack or Discord notification webhook;
  - in a workspace, deleting it, changing someone's role, removing someone and inviting an admin.

  Signing out your other devices never asks. Signing in also counts as confirming for 10 minutes. With two-step verification on, that's only when you answered the second step.
- **Turning on two-step verification signs out your other devices.**
- **Each backup code you use is emailed to you,** with how many are left.

## Safer sign-up and recovery

- Google and GitHub sign-ups need an email address the provider has verified.
- If someone started an account with your address before you, proving the address with an email code removes their passkeys, two-step verification, trusted devices, API keys and app access.
- When support turns off two-step verification for you, it now waits 7 days and emails you the date. Sign in with your authenticator app or a backup code, or click **Cancel** in Settings, to stop it.
