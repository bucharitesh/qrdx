Ritesh Bucha is the founder of QRdx and its main maintainer. He builds the QRdx web app, the `qrdx` React component on npm and the `@qrdx/cli` command-line tool, all developed in the open at [github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx](https://github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx). He writes here about how QR codes work, how QRdx is built and what ships next.
