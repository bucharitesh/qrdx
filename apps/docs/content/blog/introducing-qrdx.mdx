QRdx is an open-source QR code generator with AI theme generation. You can use it to make QR codes for a brand, a business or a project, and customize every part of them.

## AI theme generation

<Figure
  src="/blog/introducing-qrdx/ai-styles.svg"
  alt="Line drawing of a short text prompt card linked by dotted lines to three QR codes in different styles"
  width={1600}
  height={1067}
  caption="Describe a style in plain words and QRdx turns it into a QR code theme you can keep editing."
/>

Describe the style you want, such as minimal, bold, retro or futuristic, and QRdx's AI turns the description into a QR code theme in seconds.

The playground shows a live preview, so you see each change as you make it.

## Full customization

<Figure
  src="/blog/introducing-qrdx/layers.svg"
  alt="Exploded line drawing of a QR code's layers above a base: the paper, the data modules, the corner eyes, and a frame with a label bar"
  width={1600}
  height={1200}
  caption="Every layer is independent: background, dot pattern, corner eyes, logo and frame."
/>

You can change each part of the code:

- solid colors or gradients for the body, the eyes and the background;
- the body and eye patterns, from a range of shapes;
- a logo in the center;
- a call-to-action frame with your own text;
- the download format: PNG, JPG or SVG, at any resolution.

## Open source

<Figure
  src="/blog/introducing-qrdx/open-source.svg"
  alt="Line drawing of a QR code on a stand, a dashed copy of it behind, and a branch diagram on the floor suggesting a fork"
  width={1600}

  caption="The code is on GitHub, so you can read it, fork it and self-host it."

The QRdx app is open source under AGPL-3.0, and the `qrdx` React library is MIT-licensed from version 0.0.47. You can clone the app, change it and run your own instance, or add the `qrdx` React component to your own app.

The repository is [github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx](https://github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx).

It's built for designers making branded codes, businesses that print their brand on packaging, and developers building their own QR platform.
