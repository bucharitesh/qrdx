Flam makes its branded QR codes with qrdx, the open-source library behind QRdx. Each code is round, sits inside a ring of text, and gets a pattern of dots that belongs to its link alone.

## Who Flam is

[Flam](https://www.flamapp.ai) builds "AI models and products for interactive content", with formats called Flicks, Airboards and Visual Agents.

## What Flam needed

A QR code in its own style, not a stock square. Codes for many different links that still look like one family. And files ready for print and design: PNG, JPG and SVG, including PNGs with a transparent background.

## What Flam built

A small generator inside its own Next.js app, using qrdx and a custom template. qrdx draws the code, and the template draws everything around it: a dark ring with text along it, and a halo of dots seeded by the link. The template picks 240 of 297 dot positions for each link, which allows about 10<sup>61</sup> different rings, so two links practically never share one, and the same link always gets the same ring.

The repository's first commit, on November 11, 2025, already held a working generator.

## Why QRdx

- **Full control.** A template is code, so the design is exactly Flam's, not a preset with a logo dropped in.
- **One design everywhere.** The preview, the PNG, JPG and SVG downloads and the copied SVG all come from the same template.
- **Nothing to pay per code.** The library is MIT-licensed and runs inside Flam's app, with no QRdx account, no calls to QRdx's servers and no limit on how many codes it makes.

## Make your own

QRdx now has this design built in as the **Dot Ring** template: the ring, the text along it, and a dot halo that's different for every link.
