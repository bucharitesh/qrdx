This guide ranks free QR code generators in 2026 against six criteria:

1. No signup required to create and download
2. Unlimited QR codes on the free tier
3. No watermarks on downloaded images
4. No scan limits
5. Customization (colors, logos, patterns)
6. Analytics (scan or click tracking)

## 1. QRdx: best overall free QR code generator

qrdx.dev covers more of the six criteria than anything else on this list: unlimited free static codes, no signup, no watermarks, logo support, and open-source code you can self-host. A free account adds 10 dynamic codes with built-in scan analytics, and scans are never capped.

**What's free:**
- Unlimited QR codes in the playground, with no account
- PNG, SVG or JPG downloads with no watermark
- Full customization: {facts.bodyPatterns} body patterns, {facts.eyeShapes} corner eyes and {facts.eyeCentres} eye centers, linear and radial gradients, logo overlay
- Click analytics through the optional Dub.sh integration: encode a Dub short link and track its clicks in Dub (requires a QRdx account and a Dub account)
- Open source on GitHub (the app is AGPL-3.0, the `qrdx` library is MIT from 0.0.47), so you can read the code or run your own instance

**Who it's for:** designers who want full creative control, businesses that need branded QR codes, and developers who want an embeddable TypeScript React component (`npm install qrdx`) instead of a SaaS dashboard.

**Limitations:** the free plan caps dynamic codes at 10, analytics history at 90 days and saved themes at 10, and includes only a few free AI theme requests. Pro ($5/mo) raises those limits. Static generation and downloads stay unlimited and free.

## 2. QRKIT: best for simple static QR codes

useqrkit.com has unlimited static QR codes with no signup and 15+ QR types. It's fast and plain, a good fit if you need a standard QR code with basic customization.

**What's good:**
- Unlimited static QR codes, no account
- 5 free dynamic codes with account creation
- Supports URL, WiFi, vCard, email, phone, and more

**What's missing:** scan analytics on the free tier, a developer API and open-source code.

## 3. QR Code Monkey: best for high-quality static QR codes

qrcode-monkey.com has been around since 2012 and generates high-resolution static QR codes with good customization options.

**What's good:**
- Unlimited free QR codes (static only)
- Logo support
- Multiple dot patterns
- High-resolution downloads

**What's missing:** dynamic QR codes need a paid plan. There are no analytics and no developer API, and it's closed-source SaaS.

## 4. QR-Verse: best SaaS alternative for non-developers

qr-verse.com is a newer entrant with unlimited dynamic QR codes and full analytics. It needs an account but sets no hard limits on the free tier.

**What's good:**
- Unlimited dynamic QR codes
- Full scan analytics (location, device, count)
- 20+ QR types
- AI art QR code generation

**What's missing:** you need an account, so there's no anonymous generation. There's no open-source code and no developer npm package or API.

qrcode-tiger.com is one of the most heavily marketed QR code generators, but its free tier is barely usable:

- Only 3 QR codes on the free tier
- 500 scans per code (then the QR stops working unless you upgrade)
- Ads shown on the QR page redirect

Against QR Tiger's free tier, QRdx has more codes, no scan limits and free logo support. QR Tiger's paid plans add dynamic codes and scan analytics; QRdx includes both on a free account, for up to 10 dynamic codes.

## 6. Flowcode: very limited free tier

flowcode.com limits the free tier to 2 QR codes with 500 total scans before you have to upgrade. It suits enterprise marketing teams better than individual users.

## 7. Canva QR Code Generator

Canva makes basic static QR codes inside its design tool. It needs an account, and the QR feature only produces standard square codes with no pattern customization or analytics. It's useful if you're already designing in Canva and need a plain QR code, but it isn't a standalone QR code tool.

**You're a designer or business** making branded QR codes for print, packaging or marketing materials: use QRdx. Patterns, gradients and logos are free, and the playground previews each change as you make it.

**You're a developer** who needs to generate QR codes programmatically: QRdx ships `qrdx`, a typed React component on npm, plus a CLI (`@qrdx/cli`), so you can render styled codes in your own app instead of calling a SaaS. If you only need plain codes, established open-source libraries such as `qrcode` work well too.

**You just need a plain QR code fast:** use QR Code Monkey or QRKIT. Both are fast and free and need no account.

**You need scan analytics on a free tier:** use QR-Verse, which includes unlimited dynamic codes with scan analytics, or a free QRdx account, which includes scan analytics on up to 10 dynamic codes.
