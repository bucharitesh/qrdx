QRdx now has **Integrations**: you can connect other tools you use to your QRdx account, starting today.

## What are QRdx integrations?

An integration connects a third-party tool to your QRdx account and adds features to the QRdx editor and dashboard.

Both integrations connect through **OAuth 2.0**, so you never give us a password. You grant access, QRdx stores the tokens encrypted at rest on its servers, and it refreshes them in the background before they expire.

You can find, connect and manage integrations under **Settings → Integrations**.

## The first two integrations

### Dub.sh: short links and link analytics

[Dub.sh](https://dub.co) is an open-source link management platform. With the Dub integration, you can make short links for your QR codes inside the QRdx editor.

Once it's connected, you can:

- turn any long URL into a short link;
- see each link's clicks, referrers, devices and locations;
- use your own domain, connected through your Dub workspace;
- set a date when a link expires;
- create a short link and its QR code in one step.

The integration uses Dub's OAuth 2.0 flow with PKCE. You give QRdx access to your Dub workspaces and links, and it asks for four scopes: `workspaces.read`, `workspaces.write`, `links.read` and `links.write`.

### Google Drive: import images from your Drive

If your logo or brand images are in Google Drive, the Drive integration lets you browse and import them without downloading and re-uploading.

Once it's connected, you can:

- browse your Drive images in a file picker inside QRdx;
- use any Drive image as a logo or background;
- search your files as you type;
- see a thumbnail before you import.

## How OAuth works in QRdx

<Figure
  src="/blog/introducing-qrdx-integrations/oauth.svg"
  alt="Line drawing of a padlock, a consent screen with two buttons, a key on the path between them, and a QR code on a stand"
  width={1600}
  height={900}
  caption="You approve access on the provider's own page; QRdx only ever receives a scoped token, never your password."
/>

When you connect an integration:

1. Click **Connect** on an integration in your settings.
2. You're redirected to the provider's OAuth authorization page (Dub, Google, etc.).
3. You grant QRdx the permissions it asks for, which are limited to what the integration needs.
4. You're redirected back to QRdx and the connection is set up.

Tokens are encrypted at rest and never sent to the browser. When a token is close to expiring, QRdx refreshes it in the background, so you don't have to reconnect.

Each integration is a self-contained module with a config, a typed API client and a UI layer, so adding a new provider means implementing one standard interface.

We plan to open integrations to community contributions. If there's a tool you want connected to QRdx, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx).

## What's next?

We're looking at integrations with:

- Notion, to pull page content or brand assets into your QR code metadata;
- Cloudinary, to use your existing media library as a logo source;
- Zapier, to create QR codes from events elsewhere in your stack;
- analytics providers, to send link-click events to your own data pipeline.

To ask for an integration or build one, open an issue or start a discussion on GitHub.
