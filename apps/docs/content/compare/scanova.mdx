Scanova and QRdx both make dynamic QR codes with unlimited scans, hosted pages and analytics. Scanova sells annual plans priced by the number of dynamic codes, after a 14-day trial. QRdx has a permanent free plan and monthly or yearly paid plans with unlimited codes.

## Who each tool is for

Scanova suits organisations that buy software annually and want a vendor with an Enterprise tier. Its list prices are annual: Basic is $5 a month ($60 a year) for 3 dynamic codes, Lite $9 a month ($108) for 10, Standard $35 a month ($420) for 50 and Pro $75 a month ($900) for 500, with 5 users and 1 custom domain included on Pro.

QRdx suits individuals, small businesses and teams that want to start free and grow without per-code pricing. Free includes 10 dynamic codes and 3 hosted pages; Pro ($5/mo or $48/yr) makes both unlimited; Business ($15/mo or $144/yr) adds unlimited members, webhooks, conversion tracking and white-label pages.

## Where Scanova is the better choice

- **Enterprise procurement.** If your organisation needs an Enterprise contract with a custom quote, Scanova offers one. QRdx has self-serve plans and a self-hosting option, not a sales-led Enterprise tier.

## Key differences

- **Trial versus free plan.** Scanova's pricing page states that a dynamic QR code will stop working once the free trial ends. QRdx has no trial, and under the [Never-Hostage Guarantee](/guarantee) dynamic codes keep redirecting on every plan, including after a downgrade or cancellation.
- **Billing.** Scanova lists annual prices only. QRdx bills monthly or yearly.
- **Per-code pricing.** Scanova's plans are defined by dynamic code count (3 to 500). QRdx Pro and Business have no code limit.
- **Extra seats and domains.** Scanova charges $60 per extra user and $120 per extra domain a year on Pro. QRdx Business includes unlimited members and 25 domains.
- **Openness.** QRdx is AGPL-3.0 open source and can be self-hosted. Scanova is closed source.

## Switching from Scanova

1. Find out which Scanova codes are in print and whether they stay active on your current plan; they redirect through Scanova.
2. Create QRdx codes for new print runs. Existing Scanova short links can't be moved to QRdx.
3. Rebuild hosted pages in the QRdx page editor and test each code on real phones before printing.
