ME-QR and QRdx take opposite approaches to "free". ME-QR's free plan is generous with dynamic codes (up to 10,000 created, with analytics and a year of history) and pays for it with ads: its pricing page lists free codes as "with ads", and its FAQ says the first 10 scans are ad-free, after which visitors may see a short advertisement before your content. QRdx is free with no ads at all: static codes open your content directly, and its dynamic codes (10 on the Free plan) redirect straight to your destination.

## Who each tool is for

ME-QR fits people who want editable codes and scan statistics without paying, and who are comfortable with ads appearing for their audience. Its paid Lite plan includes one ad-free code; Premium removes ads from all codes.

QRdx fits anyone printing codes for customers, guests or clients who should land on the content immediately, and developers who want to generate codes inside their own product.

## Where ME-QR is the better choice

- You need **thousands of editable codes for free**, and ads are acceptable. ME-QR's free plan allows up to 10,000; QRdx Free includes 10.
- You want a huge **frame library**: ME-QR advertises 1,000+ frame options, including holiday themes. QRdx has six templates.

## Key differences

- **Ads:** ME-QR's free codes can show ads to scanners. QRdx never does.
- **Code model:** ME-QR codes are dynamic and resolve through ME-QR. QRdx offers both: static codes that need no service, and dynamic codes that redirect without ads on every plan.
- **Design:** QRdx gives separate control over 9 body patterns, 9 eye shapes and 11 eye-dot shapes with linear and radial gradients. ME-QR offers dotted and shaped codes, logos and a large frame catalog.
- **Openness:** QRdx is open source (AGPL-3.0) with an npm package.

## Switching from ME-QR

1. Leave existing ME-QR codes in place; they redirect through ME-QR, so deleting them or the account breaks printed codes.
2. For new codes, design them in the [QRdx playground](/playground). Point static codes straight at your destination, or create a dynamic code in the dashboard if you need to change it later.
3. Test every printed code on a few phones.
