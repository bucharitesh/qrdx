Dub and QRdx are both open source under AGPL-3.0, and both can turn a short link into a QR code with click or scan analytics. They start from opposite ends. Dub is a link management platform, used for marketing attribution and conversion tracking, where QR codes are one output among many. QRdx is a QR code platform, where design, print output and hosted mobile pages come first and links are the plumbing.

## Who each tool is for

Dub suits growth and marketing teams that manage large numbers of links across channels and want to tie clicks to sign-ups and revenue. It has device and geo targeting, A/B tests, conversion tracking and webhooks, and its API is on every plan. Pro is $27 a month for 12,000 new links and 600,000 tracked events a year; Business is $81 a month.

QRdx suits anyone whose links end up printed: menus, packaging, signs, business cards and posters. Free includes 10 dynamic codes and 3 hosted pages with unlimited scans; Pro ($5/mo or $48/yr) makes both unlimited and adds routing, A/B splits, UTM tagging and custom domains; Business ($15/mo or $144/yr) adds conversion tracking, webhooks and unlimited members.

## Where Dub is the better choice

- **Link attribution at scale.** If most of your links are shared online rather than printed, and you need to attribute sign-ups and sales to them, Dub is built for that.
- **Custom domains on the free plan.** Dub includes 3 custom domains for free; QRdx's start on Pro.
- **Very large link volumes.** Dub's higher plans are sized for hundreds of thousands of new links a year.

## Key differences

- **QR quality and print.** QRdx has deep QR styling, AI art, a scan checker and print-ready CMYK vector export. Dub generates QR codes for its links, without CMYK print export or AI art.
- **Hosted pages.** QRdx codes can open hosted mobile pages. Dub links redirect to pages you host elsewhere.
- **Metering.** Dub limits new links and tracked events per plan. QRdx limits only Free dynamic codes and never limits scans.
- **Price.** QRdx Pro is $5 a month; Dub Pro is $27 a month.

## Using Dub and QRdx together

You don't have to choose. Connect Dub through the [QRdx integration](/integrations/dub) or use the [Dub.sh QR code generator](/qr-code-generator/dubsh): design the code in QRdx, encode the Dub short link, and keep Dub as the source of truth for destinations and attribution. Codes that encode a Dub link depend on your Dub account, while QRdx's own dynamic codes are covered by the [Never-Hostage Guarantee](/guarantee).
