Bitly is first a link shortener and link-management platform; QR codes are one feature among links, landing pages and analytics. QRdx is built around the QR code itself: design, print output, hosted pages and scan analytics, with a free plan and an open-source codebase.

## Who each tool is for

Bitly fits teams that already manage short links in Bitly and want QR codes tied to those links and their click history. Its plans are Free (2 QR codes a month, with ads), Core ($10/mo billed annually, 5 codes a month, 30 days of click and scan data), Growth ($29/mo billed annually, 10 codes, about 4 months of data) and Premium ($199/mo billed annually, 200 codes, 1 year of data plus city and device data).

QRdx fits anyone who needs more than a couple of codes, wants full control over the design, or wants to render codes inside their own app.

## Where Bitly is the better choice

- Your links already live in Bitly and you want QR scans and link clicks in one report.
- Non-technical teammates need a single dashboard for links and codes.

QRdx's dynamic codes use QRdx short links for scans, but QRdx isn't a general link shortener for links you share online. For that, it integrates with [Dub](/integrations/dub), a link-management platform.

## Key differences

- **Volume:** Bitly meters QR codes per month. QRdx doesn't meter static codes or scans; Free accounts are limited to 10 active dynamic codes, and Pro ($5/mo or $48/yr) has no limit.
- **Design:** Bitly's free plan has limited colors and frames and no pattern customization. QRdx's free tier has every pattern, gradients, logos, six templates and PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF and EPS export.
- **Code model:** Bitly codes are tied to Bitly links. QRdx offers static codes, which need no service, and dynamic codes on QRdx short links, which keep redirecting on every plan.
- **Developers:** QRdx has a REST API and an MCP server, and is also an npm package with TypeScript types, so you can render the same codes in your own app.

## Switching from Bitly

1. Keep Bitly codes that are already printed on an active account; they resolve through Bitly links.
2. Recreate your brand style in the [QRdx playground](/playground) and save it as a theme.
3. Encode the final URL directly in a static code, or use a QRdx dynamic code if you need to change it later.
4. Test each new code on a few phones before you print.
