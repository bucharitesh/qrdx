Flowcode is a marketing platform built around tracked QR codes. Each Flowcode points to a redirect, often to a Flowcode landing page, and the value is in the analytics, audience data and integrations behind it. QRdx is a design-first static QR code generator: you style the code, download it, and it works forever with no service in between.

## Who each tool is for

Flowcode fits brands running campaigns who want scan data, landing pages and CRM connections in one product. Its self-serve plans are Free (up to 2 Flowcodes, analytics for 500 scans), Pro Plus ($25/mo billed annually, up to 50 Flowcodes and landing pages, analytics for 6,000 scans) and Growth ($250, annual only, starting at 150 Flowcodes with geo-location analytics, CRM integrations and API access). Larger plans are usage-based or custom.

QRdx fits designers, small businesses and developers who want polished static codes without an account or subscription, and developers who want to render codes in their own app.

## Where Flowcode is the better choice

- You need **landing pages** and scan analytics in the same tool.
- You need **CRM or CDP integrations** and audience retargeting.
- You want a managed platform with sales support for large campaigns.

QRdx has no landing pages or audience features. If you only need editable links and click counts, QRdx can encode a [Dub](/integrations/dub) short link and leave tracking to Dub.

## Key differences

- **Code model:** Flowcodes are tracked through Flowcode's service. QRdx codes are static by default, so they keep working whatever happens to any account.
- **Free tier:** Flowcode's free plan allows 2 codes. QRdx allows unlimited codes with the full editor.
- **Developer access:** Flowcode's API starts on Growth. QRdx is an npm package with React components, plus a CLI.
- **Openness:** QRdx is open source (AGPL-3.0) and self-hostable.

## Switching from Flowcode

1. Leave printed Flowcodes on an active plan until you reprint them; they redirect through Flowcode.
2. Design new codes in the [QRdx playground](/playground) and save the style as a theme.
3. Point each code straight at its destination, or at a Dub link if you still want to change it later.
4. Test on several phones before printing.
