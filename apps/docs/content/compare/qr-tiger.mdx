QR TIGER and QRdx look similar at first glance, but they are built for different jobs. QR TIGER is a dynamic QR code platform: codes point to a short redirect you can change later, every scan is tracked, and plans are priced by how many dynamic codes you need. QRdx is a static QR code generator: your content is encoded directly in the code, so it never expires and works without any service in between.

## Who each tool is for

QR TIGER fits marketing teams that print codes on packaging, posters or menus and need to change destinations and report on scans. Its plans range from Regular (from US$7, 12 dynamic codes) to Advanced (US$16 billed annually, 200 codes, bulk generation), Premium (US$37 billed yearly, 600 codes) and Professional (US$89, up to 1,200 codes).

QRdx fits designers and developers who want beautiful static codes with no account, no limits and no subscription, and developers who want to render those codes inside their own product.

## Where QR TIGER is the better choice

- You need **many editable codes** with scan reports in one dashboard.
- You need **bulk generation** from a spreadsheet.
- You want a hosted **QR API** rather than a client-side library.

QRdx can make a code editable by encoding a [Dub](/integrations/dub) short link, with destination changes and click analytics handled in Dub, but it is not a full dynamic QR platform.

## Key differences

- **Pricing model:** QR TIGER charges by number of dynamic codes and API requests. QRdx is free; Pro ($8/mo or $79/yr) only adds unlimited AI theme generation and saved themes.
- **Free tier:** both allow unlimited static codes. QR TIGER adds 3 dynamic codes capped at 500 scans each.
- **Developer access:** QR TIGER's API allows 500 requests a month on Regular, 3,000 on Advanced and 10,000 on Premium. QRdx is an npm package you run yourself, with no quotas.
- **Openness:** QRdx is open source (AGPL-3.0) and self-hostable. QR TIGER is not.

## Switching from QR TIGER

1. Keep your QR TIGER plan for codes already in print. Dynamic codes redirect through QR TIGER's servers, so check what happens to them before you cancel.
2. For new codes, design them in the [QRdx playground](/playground) and save the look as a theme.
3. If you still need editable destinations, connect Dub and encode a Dub link instead of the final URL.
4. Test every code on real phones before printing.
