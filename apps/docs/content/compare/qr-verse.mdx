QR-Verse is a freemium QR service with dynamic codes, analytics and AI-generated QR art. Its free plan is a starting point (one dynamic code, basic styling, PNG downloads), and the useful extras are on Pro and Business. QRdx keeps all design and export features on its free tier and is open source.

## Who each tool is for

QR-Verse fits people who want cheap editable codes and AI art in one place. Its Pro plan is €3.99/mo billed annually (€47.90/yr) or €4.99 monthly, with unlimited dynamic codes, full analytics history, logo upload, PNG, SVG and WebP downloads, AI art (5 a day, 30 a month) and API access. Business (€10.49/mo yearly or €12.99 monthly) adds white-label branding, custom domains, bulk generation and team members.

QRdx fits people who want free, high-quality static codes with logos and vector export, and developers who want to render codes in their own product.

## Where QR-Verse is the better choice

- You want **AI-generated artistic QR codes**. QRdx's AI generates color and pattern themes, not illustrated QR art.
- You need **many editable codes** with analytics at a low price.
- You want white-label scan pages and custom domains (Business plan).

## Key differences

- **Free tier:** QR-Verse's free plan has one dynamic code, basic colors and patterns, no logo and PNG only. QRdx's free tier has unlimited static codes with every design option and four export formats.
- **Editable codes:** QR-Verse has them natively. QRdx can encode a [Dub](/integrations/dub) short link so you can change the destination and track clicks in Dub.
- **AI:** both have AI features. QRdx gives 5 free AI theme generations with an account and unlimited on Pro ($8/mo).
- **Openness:** QRdx is open source (AGPL-3.0) and self-hostable.

## Switching from QR-Verse

1. Keep your QR-Verse dynamic code active while it is in print.
2. Design new codes in the [QRdx playground](/playground), add your logo, and save the style as a theme.
3. Export SVG for print or PNG for screens, then test on a few phones.
