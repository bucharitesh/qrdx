QR-Verse is a freemium QR service with dynamic codes, analytics and AI-generated QR art. Its free plan is a starting point (one dynamic code, basic styling, PNG downloads), and the useful extras are on Pro and Business. QRdx keeps all design and export features on its free tier, includes 10 dynamic codes on the Free plan, and is open source.

## Who each tool is for

QR-Verse fits people who want cheap editable codes and AI art in one place. Its Pro plan is €3.99/mo billed annually (€47.90/yr) or €4.99 monthly, with unlimited dynamic codes, full analytics history, logo upload, PNG, SVG and WebP downloads, AI art (5 a day, 30 a month) and API access. Business (€10.49/mo yearly or €12.99 monthly) adds white-label branding, custom domains, bulk generation and team members.

QRdx fits people who want free, high-quality codes with logos and vector export, editable codes with unlimited scans, and developers who want to render codes in their own product.

## Where QR-Verse is the better choice

- You want **AI QR art from a very large style catalogue**. QR-Verse advertises 157 styles; QRdx's AI art has a smaller set of presets.

## Key differences

- **Free tier:** QR-Verse's free plan has one dynamic code, basic colors and patterns, no logo and PNG only. QRdx's free tier has unlimited static codes with every design option, five export formats and 10 dynamic codes.
- **Editable codes:** both have them natively. QRdx's Free plan includes 10 dynamic codes and Pro ($5/mo or $48/yr) makes them unlimited, with unlimited scans on every plan.
- **AI:** both have AI features. QRdx gives 5 free AI theme generations with an account and unlimited on Pro ($5/mo), and makes AI QR art with a monthly allowance on every plan.
- **Openness:** QRdx is open source (AGPL-3.0) and self-hostable.

## Switching from QR-Verse

1. Keep your QR-Verse dynamic code active while it is in print.
2. Design new codes in the [QRdx playground](/playground), add your logo, and save the style as a theme.
3. Export SVG for print or PNG for screens, then test on a few phones.
