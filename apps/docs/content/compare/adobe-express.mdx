Adobe Express is a general design app, and its free QR code generator is a convenient extra: pick a template, set colors, add a logo and frame, and download. QRdx is built only for QR codes, with more control over the code itself and a library for developers.

## Who each tool is for

Adobe Express fits people who are making a flyer, social post or poster in Express and want the code designed alongside it. Its QR generator is free in the browser, creates static codes (the destination cannot be changed after creation), and downloads as PNG, JPEG or PDF. Express itself has a free plan with no credit card and a paid Premium plan for extra templates, fonts and generative credits.

QRdx fits people who want a standalone, distinctive code with vector export, people who need codes they can edit and track after printing, and developers who need to render QR codes in their own apps.

## Where Adobe Express is the better choice

- You want the code designed together with the rest of a layout in one Adobe file.
- You already use Adobe's templates, fonts and brand assets.
- You want generative AI image tools next to the code; Express Premium includes generative credits.

## Key differences

- **Formats:** QRdx exports SVG and EPS as well as PNG, JPG and PDF. Adobe Express lists PNG, JPEG and PDF for QR codes.
- **Code styling:** QRdx gives you separate control over body pattern, eye shape and eye-dot shape, plus gradients. Express offers templates, colors, a logo and frames.
- **Editable links:** Adobe Express codes are static, so the destination can't change after creation. QRdx also makes dynamic codes you can edit after printing, with scan analytics: 10 on the Free plan and unlimited on Pro ($5/mo or $48/yr).
- **Developers:** QRdx is open source (AGPL-3.0) with a REST API, an npm package and a CLI.

## Switching from Adobe Express

1. Open the [QRdx playground](/playground) and enter the same content. Static codes from Express keep working, so there is no rush to replace printed ones.
2. Rebuild your colors and logo, then save the style as a theme for reuse.
3. Export SVG for print, place it in your layout, and test the printed result on a few phones.
