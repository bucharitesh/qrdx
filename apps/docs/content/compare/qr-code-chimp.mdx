QR Code Chimp is a full dynamic QR platform: codes redirect through its service, scans are counted against a monthly allowance, and plans add more codes, higher download resolutions and digital business cards. QRdx is a static QR code generator and React library: it focuses on designing the code itself, and the code works without any service behind it.

## Who each tool is for

QR Code Chimp fits businesses that want many editable codes, scan analytics, folders, team accounts and NFC-compatible digital business cards in one place. Its free plan includes 10 dynamic codes, 1,000 scans a month, basic analytics and downloads up to 1024×1024. Paid plans billed yearly are Starter ($6.99/mo, 50 codes), Pro ($13.99/mo, 300 codes) and Ultima ($34.99/mo, 900 codes, unlimited scans); monthly billing costs $9.99, $19.99 and $49.99.

QRdx fits people who want distinctive static codes with no limits or account, and developers who want codes rendered in their own product.

## Where QR Code Chimp is the better choice

- You need **lots of editable codes** with scan analytics and folders.
- You want **digital business cards** alongside your QR codes.
- You need multiple team accounts on one plan.

## Key differences

- **Limits:** QR Code Chimp meters dynamic codes, scans, resolution, folders and accounts by plan. QRdx static codes have none of those limits.
- **Export:** QRdx exports PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF on the free tier, at custom sizes.
- **Editable links:** QRdx can encode a [Dub](/integrations/dub) short link so you can change the destination and see clicks in Dub, but it has no business cards or landing pages.
- **Openness:** QRdx is open source (AGPL-3.0) with an npm package and CLI.

## Switching from QR Code Chimp

1. Keep printed dynamic codes on an active plan until you replace them; they redirect through QR Code Chimp.
2. Design new codes in the [QRdx playground](/playground) and save your style as a theme.
3. Encode the final URL directly, or a Dub link if it may change.
4. Export SVG for print and test each code on a few phones.
