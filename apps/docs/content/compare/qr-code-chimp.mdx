QR Code Chimp is a full dynamic QR platform: codes redirect through its service, scans are counted against a monthly allowance, and plans add more codes, higher download resolutions and digital business cards. QRdx started as a static QR code generator and React library focused on designing the code itself, and now also makes dynamic codes and hosted pages, with no scan allowance on any plan.

## Who each tool is for

QR Code Chimp fits businesses that want many editable codes, scan analytics, folders, team accounts and NFC-compatible digital business cards in one place. Its free plan includes 10 dynamic codes, 1,000 scans a month, basic analytics and downloads up to 1024×1024. Paid plans billed yearly are Starter ($6.99/mo, 50 codes), Pro ($13.99/mo, 300 codes) and Ultima ($34.99/mo, 900 codes, unlimited scans); monthly billing costs $9.99, $19.99 and $49.99.

QRdx fits people who want distinctive codes without scan limits: static codes need no account, Free includes 10 dynamic codes, and Pro ($5/mo or $48/yr) makes them unlimited. Developers can use the REST API or render codes in their own product with the React library.

## Where QR Code Chimp is the better choice

- You want **NFC-compatible digital business cards**. QRdx has vCard Plus contact pages, but no NFC cards.

## Key differences

- **Limits:** QR Code Chimp meters dynamic codes, scans, resolution, folders and accounts by plan. QRdx never limits scans or download resolution; Free accounts are limited to 10 active dynamic codes and 3 hosted pages.
- **Export:** QRdx exports PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF and EPS on the free tier, at custom sizes.
- **Editable codes:** both make dynamic codes with scan analytics. QRdx dynamic codes keep redirecting on every plan, even after you cancel, and can open hosted pages such as vCard Plus contact pages.
- **Openness:** QRdx is open source (AGPL-3.0) with an npm package and CLI.

## Switching from QR Code Chimp

1. Keep printed dynamic codes on an active plan until you replace them; they redirect through QR Code Chimp.
2. Design new codes in the [QRdx playground](/playground) and save your style as a theme.
3. Use a static code for content that won't change, or a QRdx dynamic code if it may.
4. Export SVG for print and test each code on a few phones.
