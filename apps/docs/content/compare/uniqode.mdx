Uniqode is built for larger organizations: dynamic codes, digital business cards, analytics and the security documentation enterprise buyers ask for. QRdx is a free, open-source QR code generator and React library, aimed at designers and developers who want great-looking static codes without a contract.

## Who each tool is for

Uniqode fits companies that need editable codes at scale with the paperwork enterprise buyers ask for. Its pricing page lists SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, ISO, 2FA and SSO in its security section, offers a 14-day free trial, bills annually only, and starts premium features at $9/month, with its Core plan at $49/month. Static codes are free.

QRdx fits individuals, small teams and developers who want unlimited, fully styled static codes for free, or who want to self-host the generator.

## Where Uniqode is the better choice

- Your security team requires a vendor with **SOC 2 or HIPAA** documentation.
- You need **SSO** and a managed platform with vendor support.
- You need many **dynamic codes** with analytics.

QRdx does not offer compliance certifications or SSO. Its alternative is control: because it is open source, you can review the code and run it on infrastructure you already trust.

## Key differences

- **Pricing:** Uniqode's paid plans are annual only, from $9/month. QRdx is free; Pro ($8/mo or $79/yr) only adds unlimited AI theme generation and saved themes.
- **Code model:** Uniqode's dynamic codes stop working if you do not keep a subscription. QRdx codes are static unless you choose to encode a [Dub](/integrations/dub) short link.
- **Developers:** QRdx is an npm package with TypeScript types and a CLI, so codes are generated locally.

## Switching from Uniqode

1. Inventory printed dynamic codes; they need an active Uniqode plan until reprinted.
2. Design replacements in the [QRdx playground](/playground) and save your brand style as a theme.
3. Encode final URLs directly, or Dub links if destinations may change.
4. Test every code on a few phones before printing.
