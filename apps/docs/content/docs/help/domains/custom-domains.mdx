title: Use your own domain
description: Connect a custom domain, like go.yourbrand.com, for your dynamic codes' short links.
---

With a custom domain, your codes encode `go.yourbrand.com/…` instead of a `qrdx.dev` link. People see your brand before they scan. Pro includes 5 domains and Business 25.

## Connect a domain

1. Go to **Domains** in your dashboard and click **Add domain**.
2. Enter the domain, for example `go.yourbrand.com`. A subdomain works best: your main website stays where it is.
3. Add the DNS records QRdx shows at your DNS provider, the place where you bought the domain or manage its DNS. Copy the values exactly.<IfAvailable feature="domainConnect"> If you see **Set up with** and your provider's name, you can click it instead: your provider adds the records once you approve them there.</IfAvailable>
4. Click **Verify**.

The status shows **Waiting for DNS** until the records are found, then **Verified**. DNS changes usually show up within minutes, but can take up to 48 hours.

## Use it for a code

- **New code:** pick your domain in **Short link domain** before you click **Create code**.
- **Existing code:** choose the domain in **Edit link**, save, then download the code again. The encoded link has changed.

## Printed codes keep working

Every code also keeps working at its `qrdx.dev` link. Codes you printed before moving to your domain don't break.

Moving a code from one custom domain to another does break codes printed with the old domain. QRdx warns you before you save.
