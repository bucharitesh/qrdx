With a custom domain, your codes encode `go.yourbrand.com/…` instead of a `qrdx.dev` link, so people see your brand before they scan. This page gets a domain connected and onto a code; the [Custom domains guide](/docs/custom-domains) has the details.

<Callout type="info">
  Pro includes 5 domains and Business 25. Workspace owners and admins add and verify domains; every member can use a verified one. See [Plans and what they include](/docs/help/account-and-billing/plans).
</Callout>

## Connect a domain

### 1. Add the domain

Go to **Settings** → **Domains** in your dashboard and click **Add domain**. Enter the domain, for example `go.yourbrand.com`. A subdomain such as `go.` or `qr.` works best: your main website stays where it is.

### 2. Add the DNS records

QRdx shows the records to add, with their **Type**, **Name** and **Value**. Add them at your DNS provider, the place where you bought the domain or manage its DNS, copying the values exactly.<IfAvailable feature="domainConnect"> If QRdx recognizes your provider, you can click **Set up with** your provider's name instead: you approve the change there and come back.</IfAvailable>

{/* shot: domains-dns-records | the Custom domains page after adding a domain, showing the DNS records table with Type, Name and Value and the Verify button | Dashboard, Settings, Domains, Add domain */}

### 3. Verify

Click **Verify**. The status shows **Waiting for DNS** until the records are found, then **Verified**, and QRdx says you can now pick the domain for your dynamic codes. DNS changes usually show up within minutes, but can take up to 48 hours.

## Use it for a code

- **New code:** pick your domain under **Short link domain** before you click **Create code**.
- **Existing code:** open **Edit link**, pick the domain and click **Save changes**. The code now encodes the new link, so download it again. Codes you already printed with the `qrdx.dev` link keep working.

## Printed codes keep working

Every code also keeps working at its `qrdx.dev` link, so codes you printed before moving to your domain don't break.

Moving a code from one custom domain to another does break codes printed with the old domain. QRdx warns you before you save.

## If something goes wrong

### The status stays on Waiting for DNS

Check that each record's name and value match what QRdx shows, with no extra characters, then click **Check DNS**. Some providers take hours to publish a change, and a few take up to 48 hours.

### Your DNS provider didn't add the records

The automatic setup didn't go through. Add the records yourself from the table under the domain, then click **Verify**.

### Scanners see "This domain isn't connected yet"

A code already encodes the domain, but its DNS setup isn't finished. Finish the steps above; the code works as soon as the domain is **Verified**.
