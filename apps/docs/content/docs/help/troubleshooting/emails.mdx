---
title: Sign-in codes or emails don't arrive
description: What to do when the sign-in code, a team invitation or an alert email doesn't show up.
---

QRdx emails you a sign-in code, team invitations, alerts and notices about your account's security. When one doesn't show up, the cause is usually in the list below, with the most common first.

## "Your QRdx sign-in code" doesn't arrive

That's the subject of the sign-in email. The code has 6 digits and works for 5 minutes.

1. Check your spam, junk or promotions folder. Search your inbox for "QRdx sign-in code".
2. Check the address you typed. Click **Use a different email** if you made a typo.
3. Wait for **Resend** to become clickable (30 seconds), then click it. Each new code replaces the old one, so use the newest email.
4. Sign in with **Google** or **GitHub** instead, if that account uses the same email address.

On a work address, the company mail filter may hold emails from new senders. Ask your IT team to let emails from qrdx.dev through, or sign in another way.

## "Too many codes requested. Wait a minute, then try again."

You asked for several codes in a row. An address can request 5 codes every 15 minutes and 30 a day. Wait a minute, then ask once and use the newest email.

## The code is rejected

It expired after 5 minutes, or a newer code replaced it. Click **Resend** and type the code from the newest email.

## A team invitation didn't arrive

Ask the person who invited you to open **Settings** → **Team**, copy the invite link under **Pending invitations** and send it to you directly. Links expire after 48 hours and only work for the email address they were sent to. See [Create a workspace and invite people](/docs/help/teams/create-and-invite).

## Alerts or the weekly digest don't arrive

Email alerts and the digest are part of Pro and Business, and they're all off until you turn them on in **Settings** → **Notifications**. The weekly digest goes out on Mondays at 09:00 UTC, and is skipped when there were no scans in the last two weeks and no form responses.

## You got a security email you didn't expect

QRdx emails you when a new browser signs in, when two-step verification changes and when your email address changes. If it wasn't you, follow the steps in the email. See [Keep your account secure](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/account-security#emails-about-your-accounts-security).

## Still nothing

[Contact support](/docs/help/contact) and tell us the email address you're using and the time you tried.
