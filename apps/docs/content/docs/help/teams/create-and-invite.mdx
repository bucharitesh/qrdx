title: Create a workspace and invite people
description: Share codes, pages and themes with other people. More workspaces need Pro or Business.

Everything you make in QRdx belongs to a workspace. Everyone in a workspace works on the same codes, pages, files, domains and themes. This page gets a workspace made and people into it; the [Workspaces and teams guide](/docs/teams) covers how workspaces fit together.

<Callout type="info">
  Creating more workspaces and inviting people are part of Pro (up to 3 people in a workspace) and Business (no limit). See [Seats and workspace limits](/docs/help/teams/seats).
</Callout>

## Your own workspace

Every account has one, created automatically, and you're its owner. You can't leave or delete it. On Pro or Business you can invite people into it, or create another workspace for them.

## Create a workspace

1. Open the workspace switcher at the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone, in the menu).
2. Click **Create workspace**.
3. Fill in a **Workspace name** and a **Workspace URL** (3 to 48 lowercase letters, numbers and dashes).
4. Click **Create workspace**. QRdx switches you into it.

{/* shot: create-workspace-dialog | the Create workspace dialog with the Workspace name and Workspace URL fields | Dashboard, workspace switcher, Create workspace */}

## Invite someone

### 1. Open the Team page

In the workspace, go to **Settings** → **Team**.

### 2. Fill in the invitation

Under **Invite people**, enter their **Email** and choose a **Role**: **Admin**, **Member**, **Billing** or **Viewer**. Only an owner can invite a billing member. See [Team roles](/docs/help/teams/roles).

### 3. Send it

Click **Create invite link**. QRdx says it emailed the invitation and shows a link you can share yourself, with **Copy invite link**.

{/* shot: team-invite-form | the Invite people form on the Team page with an email typed, the Role picker and the Create invite link button | Dashboard, Settings, Team */}

Inviting someone as an admin asks you to [confirm it's you](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification#confirm-its-you) first, unless you did in that browser in the last 10 minutes.

## Accepting an invitation

The invited person opens the link and signs in with the same email address the invite was sent to, then clicks **Accept invitation** or **Decline**. Invite links expire after 48 hours. Until then the invitation sits under **Pending invitations** on the Team page, where **Revoke** withdraws it.

## Share a join link

A join link lets anyone who has it join the workspace, without an invitation each. On the Team page, under **Join link**, pick the role new people get under **Joins as** and click **Create join link**. For safety the link is shown only when it's made, so copy it then with **Copy join link**. **Reset link** replaces it and stops the old one working; **Turn off** stops it altogether. People who already joined stay members. Letting a join link add admins asks you to confirm it's you.

## Switch between workspaces

Use the workspace switcher to move between your own workspace and the others you belong to. Codes you create go into the workspace you're in.

## If something goes wrong

### The invitation email didn't arrive

Copy the invite link from **Pending invitations** and send it to the person directly. See [Sign-in codes or emails don't arrive](/docs/help/troubleshooting/emails).

### They can't accept the invitation

They signed in with a different email address. Invitations only work for the address they were sent to; ask them to sign in with that one, or revoke and invite the right address.

### Create invite link is disabled

The workspace has no free seat: on Pro, 3 people including pending invitations. Revoke an invitation you don't need, or ask an owner to upgrade. See [Seats and workspace limits](/docs/help/teams/seats).
