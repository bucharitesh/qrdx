The audit log lists the changes people make in a workspace: who did it, what changed and when. It's a Business feature under the QRdx commercial license. A self-hosted QRdx server needs a license key (`QRDX_EE_LICENSE`) to turn it on; see [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting#optional-services).

<Callout type="info">
  The audit log is part of the **Business** plan. A workspace has it when one of its owners or billing members is on Business. Only **owners** and **admins** can open and export it. See [Seats and workspace limits](/docs/help/teams/seats) and [Team roles](/docs/help/teams/roles).

## Open the audit log

Go to **Settings** → **Audit log** in the workspace. The newest changes come first, 50 at a time; click **Load more** at the bottom for older ones. Click the arrow on an entry to see the old and new values, like a code's old and new destination.

{/* shot: audit-log-page | the Audit log page with the Who, Category, From and To filters and a few entries, one expanded to show old and new values | Dashboard, Settings, Audit log */}

History starts when the workspace gets Business. Changes made before that, or while the workspace was on another plan, aren't recorded. If the workspace leaves Business, recording stops; the entries already kept stay until they're {facts.auditLogRetentionDays} days old, and you can see them again if the workspace comes back to Business.

## What's recorded

Each entry has the time, who made the change, what they changed and the target (a code, a page, a person, a domain). Who made the change is a person, or an API key or app acting for a person: changes made through the [REST API](/docs/developers/rest-api) with an API key show the key's name, and changes made through an app you authorized show the app's name.<IfAvailable feature="slackBot"> Codes made in Slack with `/qr track` show the linked person "via Slack", with the Slack user and workspace ids. When an owner or admin revokes a Slack account's link in **Settings** → **API keys & apps**, that's recorded under Developers.</IfAvailable>

Each entry also keeps the IP address and the device of the request, the browser and system like "Chrome on macOS". Changes the system makes, like a plan change from billing, have neither.

| Category | What's recorded |
| --- | --- |
| **Codes** | Created (including imports and copies), destination changed, renamed, design changed, short link changed, link controls changed (password on or off, start and end dates, scan limit, fallback URL), routing rules changed, paused or resumed, archived or unarchived, moved to the trash, restored, deleted for good, an earlier version restored, moved to another folder, moved to or from another workspace. Folders and tags created, renamed or deleted. |
| **Pages** | Created, published, deleted. |
| **Team** | Invitations sent and revoked, people joining with an invitation, the join link or single sign-on, role changes, people removed, people leaving, and the join link turned on, changed or turned off. Recorded however the change was made, in QRdx or through its API. |
| **Security** | "Require two-step verification" and "Require single sign-on" turned on or off (turning on single sign-on also says how many sessions it signed out), single sign-on set up, changed, verified or removed, the role new people get when they first sign in with single sign-on, and exports of the audit log. |
| **Developers** | API keys created and revoked (their name and scopes), apps authorized or revoked, webhook endpoints added, changed or deleted, and a webhook's signing secret replaced. For an endpoint the log keeps only its server, like `https://hooks.zapier.com/…`, since the rest of a webhook URL is often secret. |
| **Domains** | Custom domains added, verified or removed. |
| **Workspace** | The name or URL changed, and the workspace moving to Business (recorded as "Billing"). |

The audit log never keeps an API key, an OAuth token, a password, a webhook URL's path or query, a webhook signing secret or a single sign-on client secret or certificate. It records that one was created, replaced or removed, not its value.

Apps you authorize act on your own workspace, so authorizing or revoking an app is recorded there.

These aren't in the audit log:

- scans, form responses and analytics, which aren't changes to the workspace (see them in Analytics and Responses);
- changes to your own account, like your name, email or two-step verification.

## Filter the log

- **Who**: one person, with the API keys and apps that act for them.
- **Category**: Codes (with folders and tags), Pages, Team, Security, Developers, Domains or Workspace.
- **From** and **To**: the first and last day to show. Days are in UTC; the times on each entry are in your time zone.

## Export as CSV

Click **Export CSV** to download what the filters show, newest first. One export holds up to {facts.auditLogExportRows} entries. For more, export a shorter date range at a time.

The file has one row per change: the time in UTC, the category, the action, a sentence describing it, who made it (and the API key or app, if any), the target, the IP address, the device, and the old and new values as JSON.

Exporting is recorded too, with the filters that were used.

## How long entries are kept

Entries are kept for {facts.auditLogRetentionDays} days, then deleted. Deleting a workspace deletes its audit log with it.

The IP addresses and devices in the audit log are personal data of the people who work in the workspace. The [privacy policy](/legal/privacy-policy) says how QRdx handles them.

## If something goes wrong

### Audit log isn't in Settings

You're a member, viewer or billing member, or the workspace isn't on Business. Ask an owner or admin, or see [Plans and what they include](/docs/help/account-and-billing/plans).

### A change you expect isn't listed

It happened before the workspace was on Business, it's older than {facts.auditLogRetentionDays} days, or it's a scan, a response or a change to someone's own account, which aren't recorded.
