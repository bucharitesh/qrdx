---
title: Team roles
description: What owners, admins and members can do in a team.
---

Every team member has one role.

## Member

- Create and edit the team's codes and pages, and upload files.
- Delete their own codes, files and form responses.
- Use the team's themes and verified domains.

## Admin

Everything a member can do, plus:

- Delete any code, file or form response.
- Invite and remove members, and change their roles.
- Add themes to the team.
- Add, verify and remove custom domains.
- Manage webhooks.

## Owner

Everything an admin can do, plus:

- Make other members owners.
- Delete the team.

## Change someone's role

On the **Team** page, change the role next to their name. Only admins and owners can change roles.

## Hand over a team

There's no separate transfer button. Make the new person an **Owner**. Once there's another owner, you can leave the team.
