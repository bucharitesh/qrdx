---
title: Seats and team limits
description: How many people a team can have, and how to leave, remove someone or delete a team.
---

## How many seats you get

- **Pro**: 3 seats, including the owner.
- **Business**: unlimited seats.
- **Free**: no teams.

A team uses the best plan among its owners. Members don't need their own subscription.

## Pending invitations use a seat

An invitation takes a seat until it's accepted or revoked. To free the seat, find the invitation on the **Team** page and click **Revoke**.

## If the owners' plan changes

People already in the team stay. Only new invitations are blocked until there's a free seat.

## Remove someone

On the **Team** page, click **Remove** next to their name, then **Remove member**. Only admins and owners can remove members.

The codes and pages they created stay with the team.

## Leave a team

On the **Team** page, click **Leave team**.

If you're the only owner, make someone else an owner first. See [Team roles](/docs/help/teams/roles).

## Delete a team

Owners can click **Delete team** on the **Team** page. To confirm, type the team's URL.

Nothing stops working. Each code, page, file and domain moves to the personal workspace of the person who created it, and printed codes keep redirecting.
