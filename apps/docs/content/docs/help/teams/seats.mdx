---
title: Seats and workspace limits
description: How many people a workspace can have, and how to leave, remove someone or delete a workspace.
---

## How many seats you get

- **Pro**: 3 seats, including the owner.
- **Business**: unlimited seats.
- **Free**: 1 seat, just you.

A workspace uses the best plan among its owners. Members don't need their own subscription.

## Pending invitations use a seat

An invitation takes a seat until it's accepted or revoked. To free the seat, find the invitation on the **Settings** → **Team** page and click **Revoke**.

## If the owners' plan changes

People already in the workspace stay. Only new invitations are blocked until there's a free seat.

## Remove someone

On the **Settings** → **Team** page, click **Remove** next to their name, then **Remove member**. Only admins and owners can remove members, and nobody can be removed from their own workspace.

The codes and pages they created stay in the workspace.

## Leave a workspace

In the workspace's **Settings** → **General**, click **Leave workspace**.

If you're the only owner, make someone else an owner first. See [Team roles](/docs/help/teams/roles). You can't leave your own workspace.

## Delete a workspace

Owners can click **Delete workspace** in **Settings** → **General**. To confirm, type the workspace's URL. Your own workspace can't be deleted; it goes when you [delete your account](/docs/help/account-and-billing/delete-your-account).

Nothing stops working. Each code, page, file and domain moves to the own workspace of the person who created it, and printed codes keep redirecting. The workspace's API keys are deleted.
