QRdx hosts a mobile page for your QR code: a link list, a menu, a contact card, a coupon or one of {facts.hostedPageTypes} other types. You build it from blocks, and you can edit it at any time without reprinting the code.

<Callout type="info">
  Free includes 3 pages, with a small "Made with QRdx" footer. Pro has unlimited pages and no footer. Business adds white-label pages and custom CSS. See [Plans and what they include](/docs/help/account-and-billing/plans).
</Callout>

## Create a page

### 1. Start a new page

Go to **Pages & forms** in your dashboard and click **New page**.

### 2. Pick a type

Pick what the page is for: Link list, Business, vCard Plus, Menu, Coupon, Event, App download, Feedback & forms, PDF, Images, MP3 / Audio, Video, Social media, Product, Appointments, Payments & tips, Property listing, Pet ID tag, Medical ID, Playlist or Custom page.

{/* shot: new-page-type-picker | the New page dialog with the page types to pick from | Dashboard, Pages & forms, New page */}

### 3. Pick a template

Pick a starting point. You can change every block, color and text later.

### 4. Create it

Name the page if you like, then click **Create page**. The editor opens, and QRdx creates a dynamic QR code for the page at the same time.

You can also start from the [playground](/playground): click **Change** on the **QR code type** card and pick a type from the **Hosted pages** group.

## Edit and publish

The page editor has three tabs: **Blocks** for the content, **Design** for colors and type, and **Settings** for the name and other options.

{/* shot: page-editor | the page editor with the Blocks tab open, the phone preview and the Publish button in the header | Dashboard, Pages & forms, open a page */}

Nothing changes for visitors until you click **Publish**, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>. The header reads "Not published yet" for a new page and "Unpublished changes" once you've edited a published one.

Unpublished edits are kept in your browser, so you can close the tab and come back. When you return, a banner says you have unpublished changes from that date, with **Restore** and **Discard**.

## Get the QR code

Click **QR code** in the page editor header to open the page's code and download it. **View live** opens the published page.

If a page has no code, open its menu (**⋯**) in **Pages & forms** and choose **Create a QR code**.

## If something goes wrong

### Visitors still see the old page

You haven't published the change. Open the page and click **Publish**.

### Your edits disappeared

They're kept in the browser you edited in. Open the page in that browser and click **Restore** on the banner. Edits made in another browser or device aren't there.

### You can't create another page

You're on Free and already have 3 pages. Delete one you don't use from **Pages & forms**, or [upgrade](/pricing).
