title: Save and reuse a design
description: Save a design as a theme, reuse it on new codes and share the style with others.
---

A **theme** is a saved design: colors, dots, eyes, logo and frame. It doesn't include your link or other content, so you can reuse it on every code you make.

<Callout type="info">
  The Free plan saves up to 10 themes. Pro and Business have no limit. See [Plans and what they include](/docs/help/account-and-billing/plans).
</Callout>

## Save a design as a theme

1. In the [playground](/playground), click the heart icon (**Save design**) above the preview, or press <kbd>S</kbd>.
2. Sign in if you're asked to.
3. In the **Save QR Code Theme** dialog, name the theme and click **Save Theme**.

When you're editing a theme you already saved, the dialog offers to **Update** it or to **Save as New**.

Your themes are on the **Themes** page in your dashboard.

## Reuse a theme

On the **Themes** page, open a theme's menu:

- **Edit in playground** opens it in the editor.
- **Rename** changes its name.
- **Set as default** uses it for your new codes. For a team theme it reads **Set as workspace default**, and **Remove as default** undoes it.
- **Copy to this workspace** shares a copy with everyone in the workspace you have open.
- **Copy link** copies a link that opens the style in the playground.
- **Publish to community** shares it with everyone (see below). **Publish its frame** publishes the frame on its own.
- **Delete** removes it.

**New theme** at the top of the page starts a new one.

{/* shot: themes-page-menu | the Themes page with one theme's menu open showing Edit in playground, Rename, Set as default, Copy to this workspace, Copy link, Publish to community and Delete | Dashboard, Themes, open a theme's menu */}

## Share a style

Click the share icon (**Share design**) above the preview, or press <kbd>X</kbd>. QRdx asks you to save the design first, then gives you a link.

The link shares the style, not your content. Anyone who opens it gets your colors, dots and eyes in the playground and adds their own link.

## Publish to the community

After you save a design, **Publish to community** on the **Themes** page shares it in the [community](/community) for others to reuse.

- It must scan Great (85 or more) with a link and with Wi-Fi details. QRdx checks when you publish.
- Uploaded logos and image art are never published.
- Everything in the community is free under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): anyone can use it for anything, with no credit needed.
- Publishing makes a snapshot: later edits to your saved design don't change what you published. To update it, publish again.

## If something goes wrong

### The heart icon has turned into Publish

The theme you're editing is saved and has no unsaved changes. Change something to save a new version, or manage it on the **Themes** page.

### You can't save another theme

You've reached the Free plan's 10 themes. Delete one you don't use, or [upgrade](/pricing).

### Publishing is refused

The design doesn't scan Great with both a link and Wi-Fi details. Open it in the playground, fix the checks in [the scan score](/docs/help/designing/scan-score), save, then publish again.
