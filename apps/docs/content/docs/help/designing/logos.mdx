## Add your logo

1. In the [playground](/playground?tab=design&section=logo), open the **Design** tab, then **Logo**.
2. Turn on **Logo in the center**.
3. Pick one of the **Quick icons**, pick a brand under **Brand logos**, or click **Upload your logo**.
4. To change it later, click **Replace** or **Remove**.

Logos can be PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG files up to 1 MB. A transparent PNG or an SVG looks best.

## Use a brand's logo

**Brand logos** opens a gallery of {facts.brandLogos} brands from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org): Instagram, WhatsApp, PayPal, Spotify, Google Maps, the app stores and thousands more. Popular ones come first, and you can search by name.

- **Color**: the brand's own color, black or white.
- **Plate**: a solid shape behind the logo. A white logo always gets one, in the brand's color, and so does a very light brand color.

The logo is saved inside your design, like an upload, so the code never loads it from anywhere. Change its size, shape and plate under **Logo options**.

Each brand's mark belongs to its owner. Use one to point people to that brand, for example your Instagram profile. A few brands asked Simple Icons to remove their marks, so they aren't in the gallery.

## Set error correction to High

A logo covers some of the code's squares. Error correction rebuilds them when the code is scanned.

Open **Error correction** in the **Design** tab and choose **High** whenever a logo is on. High lets up to 30% of the code be covered.

## If the score drops

QRdx sizes the logo for you. If the **Scan score** drops after you add it, check that **Error correction** is set to **High**.

If it still struggles, try **Logo QR**, which keeps every square in place.

## Want the logo in the dots instead?

**Logo QR** paints the dots inside your logo's shape, rather than placing the logo on top. See [Blend a logo or photo into the code](/docs/help/designing/art).
