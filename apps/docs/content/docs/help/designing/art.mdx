title: Blend a logo or photo into the code
description: Logo QR and Image QR blend a picture into the dots themselves, with no AI.
---

The **Art** tab blends a picture into the dots of the code themselves, rather than placing it on top. **Logo QR** and **Image QR** work without any AI, and a scan check tests the result in your browser before you download.

## Choose a mode

1. In the [playground](/playground?tab=art), open the **Art** tab.
2. Pick **Logo QR** to color the dots inside your logo's shape, or **Image QR** to turn the whole code into your photo. **Off** turns art off.
3. Upload your own picture, or try a sample. Logos work best as a transparent PNG or an SVG. Photos work best with one clear subject.
4. Under **Strength**, choose **Subtle**, **Balanced** or **Bold**. Stronger art looks better but is harder to scan. **Fine-tune** gives you a slider.

Art works in every download format.

## Check that it scans

The **Scan check** below the art decodes your real code in your browser at three sizes. If the result isn't great, it lists what to fix.

For the best results, set **Error correction** to **High** in the **Design** tab and keep a clear border around the code.

## Why the logo is small on a short link

With Logo QR, the logo only grows as far as the code can repair. A short link makes a small code, so the logo stays small, and the smallest codes show no logo at all. **High** error correction gives it the most room.

<IfAvailable feature="aiArt" title="AI art">

[AI QR art](/ai/art) generates artwork around a code that still scans. It needs a free account.

In the playground, pick **AI art** in the **Art** tab. It paints the code you're editing. If that code is dynamic, the art encodes the short link, so you can change where it points after printing. AI art is an image: download it as a PNG, or click **Use as Image QR** to turn it into a vector code for SVG, PDF or EPS.

Each plan includes a number of AI art images a month: 3 on Free, 30 on Pro and 150 on Business. On Business the allowance is shared by everyone in the workspace. An image that fails isn't counted.

## If something goes wrong

### The scan check isn't Great

Lower the **Strength**, set **Error correction** to **High**, and pick a picture with one clear subject and strong contrast.

### The photo looks muddy in the code

Busy photos lose their shape in the dots. Crop to one subject, or try **Logo QR** with a simple shape.

### AI art says the link is too long

AI art fits a limited number of characters. Make the code dynamic first, so it holds a short link instead of the full address. See [Create a dynamic QR code](/docs/help/dynamic-codes/create).
