## Logo QR and Image QR

In the [playground](/playground), open the **Design** tab, then **Art**. Choose a mode:

- **Logo QR** colors the dots inside your logo's shape.
- **Image QR** turns the whole code into your photo.
- **Classic** turns art off.

Upload your own picture, or try a sample. Logos work best as a transparent PNG or an SVG. Photos work best with one clear subject.

Art works in every download format, and there's no AI involved.

## Balance art and scanning

Choose how strong the art is: **Subtle**, **Balanced** or **Bold**. Stronger art looks better but is harder to scan.

The **Scan check** below the art decodes your real code in your browser at three sizes. If it isn't great, it lists what to fix.

For the best results, set **Error correction** to **High** and keep a clear border around the code.
