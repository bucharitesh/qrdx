---
title: Add a frame and a call to action
description: Put a border and a short message like "Scan me" around your QR code.
---

A frame puts a border and a short message around the code, so people know what they get when they scan. Frames sit outside the code and never cover any of its squares, so they never hurt scanning.

## Add a frame

1. In the [playground](/playground?tab=design&section=frame), open the **Design** tab, then **Frame**.
2. Pick a frame. Search by name, or browse the groups: **Classic** holds the built-in frames, including Arrow, Standard Box, Square Border, Striked Box, Halloween and Dot Ring; the frame library's collections follow; **Saved** holds frames you kept. Picking a frame replaces the one you had.
3. Type your text in **Label**, or pick one of the **Label ideas**: Scan me, Scan for menu, Join Wi-Fi, Leave a review, Follow us, Pay here, Save contact or Download the app. Picking a label idea with no frame on adds the default frame.
4. Set the **Frame color**, **Text color** and **Text size**. Some frames also have a **Second line**, an **Accent color** or an **Icon**.

Arrow, Standard Box, Square Border and Dot Ring take the color of the code's dots instead of a frame color, and Striked Box and Halloween keep their own colors.

To use your own artwork, sign in and click **Upload SVG** next to the search. The SVG's shapes and colors become a frame.

## Put the code in a shape

The same picker has a **Shaped codes** group. **Dot circle** and **Heart** put the code inside a circle or a heart of decorative dots. **Silhouette** fills a shape of your own: upload a PNG, JPEG or SVG with a dark or solid shape on a light or clear background. Only its outline is kept, never the picture.

Scanners read only the code in the middle, so print a shaped code larger than a square one for the same scanning distance. A shape takes the place of a frame: picking one clears the other.

## Keep the text short

Three or four words read best from a distance. Say what people get, like "Scan for menu", rather than what to do with their phone.

## Download with the frame

The frame is part of every download, in every format. For print, see [Download and print a QR code](/docs/help/getting-started/download-and-print).

## Share your frame

A frame you uploaded or took from the library can be published on its own: click **Publish frame** in the **Frame** section. It goes to the [community](/community) under the same terms as a published design. See [Save and reuse a design](/docs/help/designing/save-a-design).

## If something goes wrong

### The Label field is disabled

No frame is on. The field reads "Pick a frame first": pick a frame, or pick a label idea, which adds one.

### Frame color has no effect

The frame you picked takes the color of the code's dots (Arrow, Standard Box, Square Border, Dot Ring) or keeps its own colors (Striked Box, Halloween). Change the **Dots** color under **Colors**, or pick another frame.

### A silhouette is refused

The shape is too thin to hold the code. Upload a picture with a thicker, more solid shape.
