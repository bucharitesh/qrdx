QRdx changes often, and every change is written down. This page says where to read them, how new work gets decided, and which things people ask for that QRdx doesn't do yet.

## What's new

Every release is in the [changelog](/changelogs), newest first, with the version number and the date. Each entry says what changed and where to find it in the app. The changelog also has a feed you can follow.

## What's next

We don't publish a roadmap with dates, and we don't promise features before they're built. When something ships, it goes in the changelog.

What people ask for shapes what we build. To ask for something:

- use the support widget on qrdx.dev, or email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev);
- or open an idea in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/qrdxinc/qrdx/discussions).

## What QRdx doesn't do today

Some things people ask about that QRdx doesn't have right now:

- **Dynamic codes for content other than links, straight from the playground.** The **Dynamic** switch is for the URL type. Use a [mobile page](/docs/help/pages-and-forms/create-a-page) for a contact card, menu, PDF or other content you want to change later.
- **App store listings for every plugin.** The [plugins page](/plugins) says how to install each one today, from a store or from source.

<IfAvailable feature="sso">

- **Automatic provisioning (SCIM).** Business workspaces can turn on [single sign-on](/docs/help/teams/single-sign-on), and require it for their domain, but removing someone from your identity provider doesn't remove them from the workspace. With single sign-on required, it does sign them out within a day.

</IfAvailable>
