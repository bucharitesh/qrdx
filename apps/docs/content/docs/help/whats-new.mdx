## What's new

Every release is in the [changelog](/changelogs), newest first.

The biggest recent release, [0.1.0](/changelogs) on 31 October 2026, made QRdx a platform: dynamic QR codes, mobile pages and forms, smart routing, teams, notifications, webhooks and a REST API.

## What's next

We don't publish a roadmap with dates, and we don't promise features before they're built. When something ships, it goes in the changelog.

What people ask for shapes what we build. To ask for something:

- use the support widget on qrdx.dev, or email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev);
- or open an idea in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx/discussions).

## What QRdx doesn't do today

Some things people ask about that QRdx doesn't have right now:

- **Two-step verification (2FA) or passkeys.** Sign-in uses Google, GitHub or email codes. See [Keep your account secure](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/account-security).
- **Dynamic codes for content other than links**, straight from the playground. Use a [mobile page](/docs/help/pages-and-forms/create-a-page) for a contact card, menu, PDF or other content you want to change later.
- **App store listings for every plugin.** The [plugins page](/plugins) says how to install each one today.
