## Email

Write to [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev).

To get an answer faster, include:

- the email address of your QRdx account;
- the short link or page link, if it's about a code or a page;
- what you expected and what happened, with a screenshot if you can.

## The support widget

On qrdx.dev, the support and feedback widget sits in a corner of the page. It appears after you allow **Functionality** cookies. Choose **Cookie settings** in the site footer to change your choice.

## Bugs and feature ideas

QRdx is open source. Report a bug or suggest a feature in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx/issues), or start a conversation in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx/discussions).

## Special requests

- **Security problems:** email support@qrdx.dev with a subject starting `[security]`. See the [security policy](/legal/security).
- **A harmful QR code:** use the [report form](/report-abuse).
- **Refunds:** email support@qrdx.dev with the date of the charge. See the [refund policy](/legal/refunds).
