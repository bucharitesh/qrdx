---
title: See your scans
description: Find how many people scanned a dynamic QR code, when, where and on which device.
---

Every scan of a dynamic code is counted. Static codes aren't tracked.

## One code

Go to **Codes** and click the code's name. The top of the page shows:

- **Total scans** and **Scans in range** (the dates you picked).
- **Unique visitors**: each visitor counts once per day.
- **Last scan**.

Below that, **QR scans and NFC taps** splits the visits between the QR code and [NFC tags](/docs/nfc) carrying the code's link. Then come charts for countries, cities, devices, operating systems, browsers and referrers, plus when people scan and in which language. On Pro and Business, codes that [ask for precise location](/docs/precise-location) also list the places scanners chose to share.

## All your codes

The **Analytics** page in your dashboard shows totals for every code, your top codes, countries, devices and languages. **Compare codes** puts up to 4 codes side by side.

## Date ranges and history

Pick 7, 30 or 90 days, 1 year, 3 years or **All time**. All time starts on the day of the code's first scan, or of the workspace's first scan on the **Analytics** page. How far back you can look depends on your plan:

- **Free**: {facts.freeScanHistoryYears} year.
- **Pro** and **Business**: all of it, including **All time**.

Scans your plan doesn't show aren't deleted. They show again after an upgrade.

## Download your scans

On a code's page, open the **Analytics** tab and click **Export CSV**. You get up to 50,000 scans for the selected dates, with the time, place, device, operating system, browser and referrer of each, whether it was a QR scan or an NFC tap, and the shared location when there is one.

## What isn't counted

Bots and link previews aren't counted. If one network scans the same code more than 20 times in a minute, the extra scans aren't counted. The code still opens every time.

## Privacy

QRdx never stores a scanner's IP address. See [What QRdx records when someone scans](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/scan-data).
