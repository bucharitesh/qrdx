Invoices come from [Polar](https://polar.sh), which sells QRdx subscriptions as merchant of record. You find them in the billing portal, which Polar runs.

## Download an invoice or receipt

1. Open the billing portal: your avatar menu → **Manage billing**, or **Settings** → **Plan & usage** → **Manage billing**.
2. Find the payment in your order history.
3. Download its invoice or receipt. The portal lets you add a company name, a tax or VAT number and a billing address to an invoice.

**Manage billing** only appears once you have a paid subscription.

## Taxes

Polar adds sales tax or VAT where it applies, and shows it at checkout.

## Refunds

Your first payment for Pro or Business, monthly or yearly, comes with a {facts.moneyBackDays}-day money-back guarantee. Email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev) from your account's email address within {facts.moneyBackDays} days of paying, and we refund it in full. You don't need to give a reason. It's once per customer, and a refund ends the paid plan right away; your codes keep working on Free.

For any other charge, such as a renewal, email us with the date of the charge and the reason. We look at those case by case.

Read the full [refund policy](/legal/refunds).

## If something goes wrong

### You need an invoice in your company's name

Add the company details in the billing portal before you download the invoice. For a yearly Business plan you can also [pay by invoice](/docs/help/account-and-billing/pay-by-invoice) from the start.

### A payment is missing from the portal

You may be signed in to a different QRdx account than the one that paid. Check the email on the receipt and sign in with that address.
