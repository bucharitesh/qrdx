---
title: Export your data
description: Download everything in your account, or export scans and form responses as CSV.
---

Everything in your account can be downloaded, on every plan: one ZIP with all of it, or a CSV of one code's scans, one form's responses or your code images. Nothing is locked behind a paid plan.

## Everything at once

1. Go to **Settings** → **Profile & security**.
2. Under **Your data**, click **Download my data**.

You get a ZIP file with your profile, codes, pages, themes and settings as JSON files, your scans as `scans.csv`, your form responses as `form-responses.csv`, and a `README.txt` that explains each file.

The export holds up to 500,000 scans and 100,000 responses. You can download it up to 3 times an hour.

## Scans of one code

Open the code from **Codes**, go to its **Analytics** tab and click **Export CSV**. You get up to 50,000 scans for the dates you picked, with the time, place, device, operating system, browser and referrer of each. See [See your scans](/docs/help/dynamic-codes/scans).

## Form responses

In **Pages & forms**, open the page's menu (**⋯**), choose **Responses**, then click **Export CSV**.

## QR code images

In **Codes**, select the codes you want, then click **Download** and pick **PNG images (ZIP)**, **WebP images (ZIP)** or **SVG images (ZIP)**. The ZIP also holds a `codes.csv` listing each file with its title, short link and destination.

<IfAvailable feature="auditLog" title="The audit log">

## If something goes wrong

### Download my data is refused

You've downloaded it 3 times in the last hour. Wait and try again.

### The ZIP doesn't hold all your scans

The full export stops at 500,000 scans. For a busy code, use **Export CSV** on the code with a shorter date range, as many times as you need.
