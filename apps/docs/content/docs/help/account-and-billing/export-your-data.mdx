---
title: Export your data
description: Download everything in your account, or export scans and form responses as CSV.
---

## Everything at once

1. Go to **Settings** → **Profile & security**.
2. Under **Your data**, click **Download my data**.

You get a ZIP file with your codes, scans, pages, form responses, themes and account details, as JSON and CSV files, plus a README that explains them.

You can download it up to 3 times an hour.

## Scans of one code

Open the code from **Codes** and click **Export CSV**. You get up to 50,000 scans for the dates you picked.

## Form responses

In **Pages**, open the page's menu (**⋯**), choose **Responses**, then click **Export CSV**.

## QR code images

In **Codes**, select the codes you want, then click **Download**. You get a ZIP of PNG or SVG files, with a CSV list of the codes.
