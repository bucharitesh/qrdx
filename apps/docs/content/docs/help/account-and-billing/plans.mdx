QRdx has three plans: **Free**, **Pro** and **Business**. This page says what each one adds. Prices, monthly or yearly, are on the [pricing page](/pricing), and the full table of limits is in [Plans and limits](/docs/plans-and-limits).

## Without an account

Static QR codes are free and unlimited. Design and download them in the [playground](/playground) with no account and no watermark.

## Free

A free account adds:

- 10 dynamic QR codes.
- 3 mobile pages, with a small "Made with QRdx" footer.
- {facts.freeScanHistoryYears} year of scan history.
- 50 form responses a month.
- 10 saved themes.

## Pro

Everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited dynamic codes and pages, with no footer.
- Your full scan history, with no time limit.
- More workspaces, with up to 3 people in each.
- Your own short link endings (custom back-halves) and 5 custom domains.
- Smart routing, A/B tests and UTM tags.
- Passwords, start and end dates, scan limits and fallback URLs.
- Precise location, retargeting pixels, and email alerts with a weekly digest.
- 1,000 form responses a month.
- Client reports: shareable stats pages with a client's branding.

## Business

Everything in Pro, plus:

- White-label pages with custom CSS.
- Unlimited members in each workspace, and 25 custom domains.
- Conversion tracking.
- Webhooks, Zapier, and Slack and Discord alerts.
- Requiring two-step verification for everyone in a workspace.
- 10,000 form responses a month.
- A client portal where clients see the folders you share with them.

## On every plan

Scans are unlimited. No plan pauses a code because of traffic, and no plan stops a printed code from working when you stop paying. See [What happens to your codes if you stop paying](/docs/help/account-and-billing/never-hostage).

## Workspaces

A workspace uses the best plan among its owners and billing members. Your own workspace uses your plan. In a workspace you share, one owner on Business gives everyone in it Business. Members don't need their own subscription.
