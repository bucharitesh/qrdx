QRdx has three plans: **Free**, **Pro** and **Business**. Compare prices, monthly or yearly, on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Without an account

Static QR codes are free and unlimited. Design and download them in the [playground](/playground) with no account and no watermark.

## Free

A free account adds:

- 10 dynamic QR codes.
- 3 mobile pages, with a small "Made with QRdx" footer.
- 90 days of scan history.
- 50 form responses a month.
- 10 saved themes.

## Pro

Everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited dynamic codes and pages, with no footer.
- 3 years of scan history.
- Teams of up to 3 people.
- Your own short link endings (custom back-halves).
- Smart routing, A/B tests and UTM tags.
- Passwords, start and end dates, and scan limits.
- Retargeting pixels and email alerts.
- 1,000 form responses a month.

## Business

Everything in Pro, plus:

- White-label pages with custom CSS.
- Unlimited scan history.
- Unlimited team members.
- Conversion tracking.
- Webhooks, Zapier, Slack and Discord.
- 10,000 form responses a month.

## On every plan

Scans are unlimited. No plan pauses a code because of traffic.

## Team workspaces

A team uses the best plan among its owners. One owner on Business gives the whole team Business. Members don't need their own subscription.
