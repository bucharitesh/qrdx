---
title: Upgrade, downgrade or switch billing
description: Move between Free, Pro and Business, or between monthly and yearly billing.
---

You upgrade from the pricing page, and change an existing subscription in the billing portal. Payments are handled by [Polar](https://polar.sh), which sells QRdx subscriptions as merchant of record.

## Upgrade from Free

1. Go to the [pricing page](/pricing).
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Click **Upgrade to Pro** or **Upgrade to Business**.
4. Sign in if you're asked to, then pay at checkout.

Your new plan starts as soon as the payment goes through, and **Settings** → **Plan & usage** shows it.

Prices show in your country's currency where QRdx has one, such as rupees in India, and checkout charges in that currency. Your first payment for a paid plan comes with a {facts.moneyBackDays}-day money-back guarantee. See [Refunds](/docs/help/account-and-billing/invoices#refunds).

## Change plan when you already pay

Plan changes happen in the billing portal, run by Polar. Open it from any of these:

- Your avatar menu → **Manage billing**.
- **Settings** → **Plan & usage** → **Manage billing**.
- The [pricing page](/pricing) → **Switch to Pro** or **Switch to Business**.

In the portal you can move between Pro and Business, switch between monthly and yearly, and update your card.

## Downgrade to Free

To go back to Free, [cancel your subscription](/docs/help/account-and-billing/cancel). Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

## What happens when you downgrade

Your codes keep working. Only new things are limited: you can't create codes or pages beyond the new plan's limits, and scan history shows what the new plan includes. See [What happens to your codes if you stop paying](/docs/help/account-and-billing/never-hostage).

## If something goes wrong

### You paid, but the dashboard still says Free

Make sure you're signed in to the account you paid with, then reload. If it still says Free after a few minutes, [contact support](/docs/help/contact) with the email on the receipt.

### Manage billing isn't in the menu

It only appears once you have a paid subscription. On Free, upgrade from the pricing page.

### Checkout shows a currency you didn't expect

Checkout charges the currency of the country you're in. Use the currency switch above the plans on the pricing page to see the prices in another currency.
