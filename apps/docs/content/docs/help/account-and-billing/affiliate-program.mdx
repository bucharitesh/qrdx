The QRdx affiliate program pays you a share of what the customers you refer pay. It runs on qrdx.dev only; a self-hosted QRdx server doesn't offer it. The [Affiliate Terms](/legal/affiliate-terms) are the full rules; this page shows where everything is.

You earn {facts.affiliateCommissionPercent}% of what the customers you refer pay in their first {facts.affiliateCommissionMonths} months as paying customers.

## Apply

1. Sign in to QRdx, or create a free account.
2. Open the [affiliate program page](/affiliates), read the terms and fill in the short application.
3. Wait for the answer. We review each application by hand, and the result shows in **Settings** → **Affiliate program**.

## Share your link

Once you're approved, **Settings** → **Affiliate program** shows your referral link with a **Copy** button. Anyone who opens it and creates a new QRdx account in that visit becomes your referral.

{/* shot: affiliate-settings-page | the Affiliate program settings page with the referral link, its Copy button and the Earnings section | Dashboard, Settings, Affiliate program */}

- They need to sign up during that visit, without reloading the page, opening QRdx in a new tab or going between the docs and the rest of the site. No cookie is used and nothing is stored on their device, so a sign-up on a later visit doesn't count.
- Your link starts with a random code. Under **Code**, click **Change once** to pick your own, 3 to 32 characters. It can't be changed again, and links with the first code keep working.
- An account that already exists can't become your referral, and an account keeps the first referral it gets.
- Your own account doesn't count, and neither does an account using your email address under another spelling or anyone in a workspace you're a member of.

## Follow your earnings

The same page shows:

- **Link visits**, counted without cookies (repeat visits count again, up to 20 a minute from one IP address), **Sign-ups** and **Paying customers**. You never see who they are.
- **On hold**: commissions still in their {facts.affiliateHoldDays}-day wait. A refund or chargeback in that time cancels the commission.
- **Payable**: commissions past their wait that haven't been paid.
- **Paid**: what we've sent you, with each payout's PayPal transaction ID.

Commissions show in the currency the customer paid in. Payouts are in US dollars.

## Get paid

Add your **PayPal email** under **Earnings**. QRdx asks you to confirm it's you before it changes, because it decides where your money goes, and emails your account's address each time it changes.

We pay by PayPal in US dollars once a month, by hand, once everything payable reaches {facts.affiliateMinPayoutUsd}. Commissions in other currencies count at the European Central Bank's reference rate on the payout date, and each payout shows the rate used. A balance below the minimum carries over. You handle the taxes on what you earn.

## If something goes wrong

### A sign-up you sent didn't count

The person signed up on a later visit, in another tab, or already had an account. Only a new account created in the same visit as the link counts.

### A commission disappeared from On hold

The customer asked for a refund or their payment was charged back within the {facts.affiliateHoldDays}-day wait, which cancels the commission.

### You weren't paid this month

Your payable balance is under {facts.affiliateMinPayoutUsd}, or no PayPal email is set. The balance carries over to the next month.

Questions about a commission or a payout: email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev).
