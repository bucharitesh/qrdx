---
title: Cancel your subscription
description: Cancel Pro or Business at any time. Your codes keep working afterward.
---

You can cancel Pro or Business at any time. Canceling stops future charges; your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, and every dynamic code keeps working on Free.

## Cancel

1. Open the billing portal: your avatar menu → **Manage billing**, or **Settings** → **Plan & usage** → **Manage billing**.
2. Cancel the subscription in the portal, which [Polar](https://polar.sh) runs.

The portal confirms the date your plan ends.

## What happens next

- Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. You aren't charged again.
- Then your account moves to Free.
- Every dynamic code keeps redirecting, and every scan is still recorded.
- If you're over a Free limit, you can't create more codes or pages until you're under it. You can still change where codes point, archive them and export them.

See [What happens to your codes if you stop paying](/docs/help/account-and-billing/never-hostage).

## Deleting your account cancels too

If you [delete your account](/docs/help/account-and-billing/delete-your-account), QRdx cancels your subscription right away. You don't need to delete your account to stop paying.

## Refunds

Canceling doesn't refund the current period. If you cancel within {facts.moneyBackDays} days of your first payment for a paid plan, you can have that payment back: email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev) from your account's email address. A refund ends the plan right away, and your codes keep working on Free.

For other charges, see the [refund policy](/legal/refunds).

## If something goes wrong

### You can't find where to cancel

Canceling happens in the billing portal, not in QRdx's settings. Open it with **Manage billing**. If the button isn't there, you're signed in to an account without a subscription.

### You were charged after canceling

A charge that went through before you canceled isn't reversed by the cancellation. Email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev) with the date of the charge and we look at it.
