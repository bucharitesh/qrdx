---
title: Cancel your subscription
description: Cancel Pro or Business at any time. Your codes keep working afterwards.
---

## Cancel

1. Open the billing portal: your avatar menu → **Manage billing**, or **Settings** → **Plan & usage** → **Manage billing**.
2. Cancel the subscription there.

## What happens next

- Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. You aren't charged again.
- Then your account moves to Free.
- Every dynamic code keeps redirecting, and every scan is still recorded.
- If you're over a Free limit, you can't create more until you're under it. You can still change where codes point, archive them and export them.

See [What happens to your codes if you stop paying](/docs/help/account-and-billing/never-hostage).

## Deleting your account cancels too

If you [delete your account](/docs/help/account-and-billing/delete-your-account), QRdx cancels your subscription straight away.

## Refunds

Cancelling stops future charges but doesn't refund the current period. If you cancel within {facts.moneyBackDays} days of your first payment for a paid plan, you can have that payment back: email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev) from your account's email address. A refund ends the plan straight away, and your codes keep working on Free.

For other charges, see the [refund policy](/legal/refunds).
