---
title: Delete your account
description: Permanently delete your QRdx account and what's in it. Your dynamic codes stop working.
---

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting your account **can't be undone**. Your personal dynamic codes stop working: scanners see "QR code not found". [Export your data](/docs/help/account-and-billing/export-your-data) first.
</Callout>

## Delete

1. Go to **Settings** → **Account**.
2. Under **Danger zone**, click **Delete account…**.
3. Type `delete` to confirm.
4. Click **Email me a code** and type the 6-digit code we send you.
5. Click **Delete account**.

## What gets deleted

Your personal workspace: its codes, scans, pages, files and domains, plus your themes, webhooks and integrations. You're signed out on every device.

## Your subscription

If you pay for Pro or Business, QRdx cancels the subscription straight away.

## Your teams

- In a team where you're the only member, the team is deleted.
- In a team with other members, another member becomes owner and keeps the team's codes and pages.

## Just want to stop paying?

You don't need to delete your account. [Cancel your subscription](/docs/help/account-and-billing/cancel) and your codes keep working on Free.
