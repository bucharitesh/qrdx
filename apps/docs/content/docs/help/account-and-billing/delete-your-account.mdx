---
title: Delete your account
description: Permanently delete your QRdx account and what's in it. Your dynamic codes stop working.
---

Deleting your account removes it and everything in your own workspace. If you only want to stop paying, you don't need this: [cancel your subscription](/docs/help/account-and-billing/cancel) and your codes keep working on Free.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting your account can't be undone. The dynamic codes in your own workspace stop working: scanners see "QR code not found". [Export your data](/docs/help/account-and-billing/export-your-data) first.
</Callout>

## Delete

### 1. Open the danger zone

Go to **Settings** → **Profile & security**. Under **Danger zone**, click **Delete account…**.

### 2. Confirm

In the **Delete your account?** dialog, type `delete` and click **Delete account**.

{/* shot: delete-account-dialog | the Delete your account? dialog with the confirmation field | Dashboard, Settings, Profile & security, Delete account… */}

### 3. Show it's you

Unless you confirmed it's you in the last 10 minutes, QRdx asks you to. What it asks for depends on whether [two-step verification](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification) is on:

- **It's on:** use your passkey, a code from your authenticator app or one of your backup codes. We don't email you a code.
- **It's off:** click **Send code** and type the 6-digit code we email you, or sign in again with a Google or GitHub account you've linked.

Your account is then deleted and you're signed out on every device.

If two-step verification is on and you've lost both your phone and your backup codes, see [Lost your phone and your backup codes](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification#lost-your-phone-and-your-backup-codes).

## What gets deleted

Your own workspace: its codes, scans, pages, files and domains, plus your themes, webhooks and integrations.

If you invited other people into your own workspace, it isn't deleted: like any shared workspace, another member becomes owner and keeps it.

## Your subscription

If you pay for Pro or Business, QRdx cancels the subscription right away.

## Your other workspaces

- In a workspace where you're the only member, the workspace is deleted.
- In a workspace with other members, another member becomes owner and keeps the codes and pages you created there.

## If something goes wrong

### The Delete account button stays disabled

Type `delete` exactly, in lower case.

### You can't do the second step

Sign in another way first, or follow [Lost your phone and your backup codes](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification#lost-your-phone-and-your-backup-codes). Support can't delete an account for you without confirming it's yours.
