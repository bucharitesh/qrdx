Every style below is drawn by the same renderer as your downloads, with its option name and the API value to copy. Each tile is decoded with [jsQR](https://github.com/cozmo/jsQR) right here in your browser, one at a time, so the **Scans** badge is a live result rather than a promise.

All of them are also tested in the package (`packages/qrdx/tests/catalog-*.test.ts`): each value decodes through a phone-camera model (contrast loss and a quarter-module blur) at 4, 6 and 9 pixels per module, next to every other body, eye and centre, at error correction L, M, Q and H, at 148 px and 1024 px, light-on-dark, with brand colours and with a centre logo; they were also checked with ZXing and in sharp renders. A few pairings are weak together; see [Pairs to avoid](#pairs-to-avoid).

## Body patterns

The shape of the data modules (`bodyPattern`). Connected styles (`liquid`, `classy`) merge neighbouring modules into one outline. `random-dots` and `mosaic` vary per module but are deterministic: the same value always draws the same code.

## Eye frames

The outer ring of the three corner markers (`cornerEyePattern`). `leaf`, `leaf-reverse`, `drop` and `petal` turn each eye so it faces the corner of the code. Each tile pairs the eye with its recommended centre.

## Frames

Call-to-action frames are drawn around the code, so the code and its quiet zone are never covered and the downloaded image grows to fit. Every frame takes `text` (up to 24 characters), `color`, `textColor` and `fontScale` (0.5 to 1.5). `banner-top-bottom`, `circle` and `menu-card` also show `secondaryText`; `gradient-border` runs from `color` to `secondaryColor`.

<QrCatalog kind="frame" />

```tsx
import { QRCode } from "qrdx";

<QRCode
  value="https://example.com"
  bodyPattern="liquid"
  cornerEyePattern="leaf"
  cornerEyeDotPattern="leaf"
  frame={{ style: "circle", text: "Scan me", secondaryText: "example.com" }}
/>;
```

## Pairs to avoid

A few pairings of shapes that scan on their own fail together in our decode tests. The playground won't let you pick them together, and the scan score warns about them (`style-pair`):

- `diamond` bodies next to `square` eyes, `pacman` next to `rounded`, and `small-square` next to `square` or `rounded` eyes (missed up close).

`circle` eyes read in both decoders we test (jsQR and ZXing) but are marked **caution**: some scanner apps miss them. The pairs ship in the package as `WEAK_BODY_EYES`, and `getStylePairRisk(props)` returns the warning for a style.

## Retired shapes

`curly` eyes and `diamond`, `star` and `banner` centres were removed because they don't scan reliably: ZXing (the decoder in most Android scanners) misses curly eyes and diamond and star centres at phone distances, and jsQR misses banner centres in every sharp render. Codes saved with them still load and render exactly as before, and the image API still draws them for existing links, but no picker, preset or AI theme offers them, and their names are kept only as deprecated values (`RETIRED_CORNER_EYE_PATTERNS`, `RETIRED_CORNER_EYE_DOT_PATTERNS`).
