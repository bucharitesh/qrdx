---
title: Make QR codes in Discord
description: Add the QRdx bot to a Discord server or to your own account, then make QR codes with /qr or from any message.
---

<IfAvailable feature="discordBot">

The QRdx bot makes QR codes inside Discord, and you don't need a QRdx account to use it. Each reply shows the code with buttons to download it as a PNG or SVG, or to open it in the [editor](/playground) and change how it looks.

</IfAvailable>

<IfAvailable feature="discordBot" title="Add the bot">

1. Open the [Discord bot](/integrations/discord-bot) page and click **Add to Discord**.
2. Discord asks where to add it:
   - **Your account:** use it in DMs, group DMs and the servers you're in.
   - **A server:** everyone in the server can use it. You need the **Manage Server** permission there.

Some servers don't let members' own apps post for everyone. There, replies from the bot on your account are visible only to you.

</IfAvailable>

<IfAvailable feature="discordBot" title="Make a code with /qr">

1. In any chat, type `/qr` and pick it from the list.
2. Type or paste a link or some text into **content**, then press Enter.

The bot replies with the code, its content as the title, and three buttons: **Download PNG** (1024 px), **Download SVG** and **Open in editor**. A link opens in the editor ready to style. Text doesn't travel in the editor link, so paste it there yourself.

To keep the code to yourself, set **private** to **True**. Only you see that reply, and Discord removes it when you dismiss it or reload.

Very long content may not fit in one QR code, or may be too long for Discord to show as an image. The bot says so in a message only you can see.

</IfAvailable>

<IfAvailable feature="discordBot" title="Make a code from a message">

On desktop, right-click a message and choose **Apps** → **Make QR code**. On mobile, long-press the message and tap **Apps** → **Make QR code**.

The code holds the first web address in the message. A message without one becomes a code of its text. Everyone in the chat sees the reply.

Replies never mention or ping anyone, whatever the message says.

</IfAvailable>

<IfAvailable feature="discordBot" title="Privacy">

QRdx doesn't save the codes you make, and the bot can't read your chats. Discord sends QRdx what you type after `/qr`, or the one message you picked for **Make QR code**.

The content goes into the image link, and Discord loads the picture from the QRdx [image API](/docs/developers/image-api). Like any link, it can end up in logs and caches, so don't make codes of passwords or other secrets.

To stop one person flooding a channel, QRdx counts the codes each Discord account makes in a minute. It also counts the codes everyone makes together, and when too many arrive in one minute, the bot says it's busy. Neither count holds any content, and both expire after that minute.

</IfAvailable>

<IfAvailable feature="discordBot" title="Remove the bot">

- **From your account:** open **User Settings** → **Authorized Apps** and click **Deauthorize** next to QRdx.
- **From a server:** someone with **Manage Server** opens **Server Settings** → **Integrations**, picks QRdx and removes it.

</IfAvailable>

## Self-hosting

The bot works on your own QRdx once you register a Discord app and set `DISCORD_APPLICATION_ID` and `DISCORD_PUBLIC_KEY`. [`plugins/discord/README.md`](https://github.com/bucharitesh/qrdx/tree/main/plugins/discord) has the steps.
