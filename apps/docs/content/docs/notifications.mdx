Notifications tell you what happens to your codes without opening the dashboard: a first scan, a milestone, a form response, a teammate's change, and a weekly summary. Choose what QRdx tells you about in [Settings → Notifications](/settings/notifications). Settings are per person and per workspace: your own workspace and every other workspace you belong to have their own switches, and in a shared workspace every member picks their own.

<Callout type="info" title="Email on Pro, chat on Business">
  Email alerts and the weekly digest are part of Pro and Business; Slack and Discord messages are part of Business. Each workspace follows the plan of its owners and billing members. Everything is off until you turn it on. See [Plans and limits](/docs/plans-and-limits).
</Callout>

In a hurry? [Sign-in codes or emails don't arrive](/docs/help/troubleshooting/emails) covers missing alerts.

## How it works

Every scan, form response and code change is an event. Notifications subscribe you to the events you care about on one channel each: email to your account's address, a Slack or Discord channel through an incoming webhook, or your own systems through [webhooks](/docs/webhooks).

| Channel | What you can get | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Weekly digest, scan milestones, form submissions, new codes, code changes | Pro and Business |
| Slack, Discord | Scan milestones, form submissions, new codes, code changes | Business |
| [Webhooks](/docs/webhooks), Zapier, Make | Every event, including each scan | Business |

## Alerts

- **Scan milestones:** a code's first scan, then 10, 100, 500, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 50,000, 100,000, 500,000, 1,000,000 scans and every million after that. The first-scan email says when, where and on which device; the Slack and Discord message names the city and country.
- **Form submissions:** the answers from a form on one of your [hosted pages](/docs/forms-and-pages#forms), with a link to all responses.
- **New codes** and **code changes:** who created a code, or changed its destination, title, design or domain. Most useful in workspaces you share with other people.

To keep a busy form or a bulk import from flooding your inbox or channel, each target gets at most 30 alerts of one kind per hour; the rest are skipped. The weekly digest still counts them, and webhooks still receive every event.

## Weekly digest

Every Monday morning (UTC) you get last week's numbers for the workspace: scans compared with the week before, unique visitors, your top 5 codes, the top countries, new codes and form responses. It's skipped when there were no scans for two weeks and no form responses.

## Email

Alert emails go to your account's email address, under the **Email to** heading in notification settings. Each one has an **Unsubscribe** link that turns off that kind of email without signing in (mail apps that support one-click unsubscribe show it too). Turn emails back on, or off, in notification settings at any time. Team invitations and other account emails are not affected by these settings.

## Slack and Discord

1. Create an incoming webhook in the chat app:
   - **Slack:** add the [Incoming WebHooks](https://slack.com/apps/A0F7XDUAZ-incoming-webhooks) app, or your own Slack app with incoming webhooks, to a channel and copy the URL (`https://hooks.slack.com/services/...`).
   - **Discord:** channel settings → **Integrations** → **Webhooks** → **New Webhook** → **Copy Webhook URL** (`https://discord.com/api/webhooks/...`).
2. In [Settings → Notifications](/settings/notifications), under **Slack and Discord** for the workspace, click **Slack** or **Discord**, paste the URL and pick the events. Adding one asks you to [confirm it's you](/docs/help/privacy-and-security/two-step-verification#confirm-its-you) first.
3. Use the send button next to the target to post a test message.

{/* shot: add-slack-target | The Slack and Discord section with a Slack target added, its events ticked and the test send button | Settings → Notifications → Slack */}

Only Slack and Discord webhook URLs are accepted. After saving, QRdx shows only the start of the URL, because anyone who has it can post to the channel. Messages never mention `@everyone`, `@here` or roles, whatever visitors type into a form.

The [QRdx bot for Slack](/docs/slack-bot) is a separate thing: it makes QR codes inside Slack. Adding it doesn't turn alerts on, and adding alerts doesn't add the bot.

## Limits and plans

If a workspace's plan no longer includes a channel, its alerts stop, but your settings are kept and you can still turn them off or remove targets. Your QR codes keep working on every plan.

## For developers

For anything beyond these alerts, such as every single scan, use [webhooks](/docs/webhooks): signed `POST` requests to your own endpoint for `scan.created`, `form.submitted` and the other events, with retries and delivery logs.

## Self-hosting

Emails are sent through [Resend](https://resend.com): set `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` (an address on a domain verified in Resend). Without them, emails are skipped and logged, and team invites fall back to copyable links.

The weekly digest is sent by `/api/cron/digest`. On Vercel, `apps/app/vercel.json` calls it every Monday with `?shard=all`, which sends every subscriber's digest in one run. With many subscribers you can instead call it hourly on Mondays without `shard=all`, so each run handles a twenty-fourth of them. Anywhere else, call it from your own scheduler with the `CRON_SECRET` from your environment:

```bash title="Terminal"
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET" "https://your-host/api/cron/digest?shard=all"
```

Unsubscribe links are signed with `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`; changing it invalidates links in emails already sent. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting#scheduled-jobs).
