<Callout title="Summary">

- These companies run parts of QRdx for us and may handle personal data while doing it: hosting, the database, email, billing, AI, file storage, spam checks, analytics and the feedback widget.
- PostHog and Featurebase only receive data about you if you allow them in **Cookie settings**; without that, PostHog gets only anonymous counts from our servers. Umami counts visits without cookies and keeps no profile of anyone.
- Services you connect yourself, such as Dub or Google Drive, are not on this list: data reaches them only because you asked for it.
- We update this page before a new subprocessor starts handling personal data.

</Callout>

**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

## Current subprocessors

| Service | What it does for QRdx | Personal data it handles | Location | Policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vercel** | Hosting, edge network and server functions. Looks up the country and city of each request from its IP address, and registers custom domains and their TLS certificates. Runs AI Gateway, which passes AI requests to Anthropic and Google and bills QRdx for them. | Everything that passes through the Service, including IP addresses of requests and AI prompts and images on their way to the model | United States, with a global edge network | [DPA](https://vercel.com/legal/dpa), [AI Product Terms](https://vercel.com/legal/ai-product-terms) |
| **Neon** | PostgreSQL database | Account data, Customer Content and End User data (scans, page events, form responses) | United States | [DPA](https://neon.com/dpa) |
| **Upstash** | Rate limiting | IP addresses and account IDs inside short-lived counters that expire within 2 days | **India (AWS Mumbai)** | [Privacy policy](https://upstash.com/trust/privacy.pdf) |
| **Polar** | Merchant of record: checkout, subscriptions, invoices and sales tax. Card payments go through Stripe. | Name, email address, billing address, payment details, tax ID, subscription history | United States | [Privacy policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy) |
| **Resend** | Sending transactional email | Recipient addresses and email content: invitations, form response notifications (which include answers), alerts and digests | United States | [DPA](https://resend.com/legal/dpa) |
| **Anthropic (Claude, through Vercel AI Gateway)** | The AI theme chat and prompt enhancement | Prompts, the conversation so far and attached images | Set by Anthropic; QRdx doesn't choose a region | [AI Gateway data controls](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/security-and-compliance/disallow-prompt-training) |
| **Google (Gemini, through Vercel AI Gateway)** | Designing AI themes and AI QR art, through the Gemini API or Vertex AI | Prompts, the conversation so far and attached images; for AI art, the code's content (as a QR image), the prompt and any reference image | Global, wherever Google has facilities | [AI Gateway data controls](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/security-and-compliance/disallow-prompt-training) |
| **Google (Web Risk)** | Checking links against Google's lists of malware and phishing sites | Destination URLs of dynamic codes and links checked with the scan checker | Global | [Google Cloud DPA](https://cloud.google.com/terms/data-processing-addendum) |
| **Cloudflare (R2)** | Storage for files you upload, when file storage is turned on | Uploaded files and anything they contain | **Asia-Pacific** | [DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) |
| **Cloudflare (Turnstile)** | Telling people from bots on the abuse report form and on hosted-page forms | IP address and browser signals | Global | [Turnstile privacy](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/) |
| **Umami (self-hosted)** | Cookieless visit counts on qrdx.dev, on our own Umami instance (open-source software we run on Vercel and Neon, listed above; Umami the company receives nothing); off when the browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control | Pages viewed (without query strings) and product actions with coarse details such as a download format. The request's IP address and browser details, used to count unique visitors without storing them; no cookies or identifiers | **United States** (Vercel; Neon US East) | [Source code](https://github.com/umami-software/umami) |
| **PostHog** | Product analytics on qrdx.dev, only with consent | Pages viewed and features used, with coarse details; your account ID, plan, sign-up date and team ID when signed in. Never email addresses, names, code contents or destinations. Without consent, only anonymous counts from our servers (a subscription started, a form submitted) | United States | [DPA](https://posthog.com/dpa) |
| **Featurebase** | Feedback and support widget on qrdx.dev, only with consent | What you type into the widget, browser details | European Union | [Privacy policy](https://help.featurebase.app/articles/4744036-privacy-policy) |

How we protect transfers out of the EEA, the UK and Switzerland is described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy#international-transfers).

## Services that aren't subprocessors

Data reaches these services only because you, or the owner of a code or page, chose to use them. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.

- **Sign-in providers:** Google and GitHub, when you sign in with them.
- **Integrations you connect:** Dub, Google Drive and, as they become available, Bitly, Rebrandly, Short.io, Dropbox, OneDrive, Box, Notion, Stripe, Cal.com, Calendly, Shopify, Google Sheets, Slack, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Spotify and YouTube. See the [integrations directory](/integrations).
- **Destinations you set up for notifications:** your own webhooks and Slack or Discord channels.
- **On hosted pages:** maps and videos the owner embeds (Google Maps, YouTube, Vimeo, Loom) and tracking pixels the owner adds (Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn).

## Changes to this list

When we add or replace a subprocessor, we update this page at least **30** days before it starts handling personal data, except when we have to act sooner to keep the Service running or secure. Customers covered by the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) can object to a change by emailing [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev) during that period.
