## The agreement

These terms are an agreement between you and **Ritesh Bucha**, an individual based in India ("QRdx", "we", "us"), the operator of QRdx, about the QRdx affiliate program. You accept them when you apply. The [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use-policy) still apply to your QRdx account, and the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) explains how we handle the data the program uses.

## Joining

- You apply from the [affiliate program page](/affiliates) while signed in to your QRdx account. One affiliate account per QRdx account.
- We review each application by hand and can accept or reject any application.
- Once you're approved, you get a referral link, and you can see your link visits, sign-ups, paying customers, commissions and payouts in **Settings** → **Affiliate program**.

## Referrals

- A referral is a new QRdx account created through your link.
- When someone opens your link and creates an account during that visit, without reloading the page or opening QRdx in a new tab, your code goes along with the sign-up. If they allowed Analytics in Cookie settings, a cookie also remembers your link for {facts.affiliateCookieDays} days, so an account they create on a later visit counts too. Without that choice, a later visit doesn't count. The [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy) lists the cookie.
- An account that already exists can't become a referral, and an account keeps the first referral it gets. It's never moved to another affiliate.
- We don't tell you who your referrals are. You see counts and amounts only.

## Commissions

- You earn {facts.affiliateCommissionPercent}% of each payment a referred customer makes for QRdx in their first {facts.affiliateCommissionMonths} months as a paying customer, counted from their first payment. Renewals, upgrades and yearly plans paid in that time all count. Payments after those {facts.affiliateCommissionMonths} months earn nothing.
- The commission is worked out on the amount the customer paid after discounts and before tax, as recorded by Polar, our payment provider and merchant of record. An order that cost nothing, such as one with a 100% discount, earns nothing.
- Commissions are kept in the currency the customer paid in and are never converted.

## Your own account doesn't count

There's no commission on payments from:

- your own account;
- an account using your email address under another spelling, such as an added `+tag`, or for Gmail, dots;
- anyone who is a member of a workspace you're a member of.

We check this when a referral is recorded and again on every payment.

## Waiting period, refunds and chargebacks

- Each commission waits {facts.affiliateHoldDays} days from the customer's payment.
- If the payment is refunded, in full or in part, or charged back within those {facts.affiliateHoldDays} days, the commission is cancelled. A refund of tax alone doesn't cancel it.
- After {facts.affiliateHoldDays} days, the commission is payable.

## Payouts

- We pay by PayPal, once a month, to the PayPal email address you add in **Settings** → **Affiliate program**. We send each payout by hand. We don't pay by any other method.
- The minimum payout is {facts.affiliateMinPayoutUsd} for commissions in US dollars and {facts.affiliateMinPayoutInr} for commissions in Indian rupees. Each currency is paid on its own. A balance below the minimum carries over to the next month.
- We pay only what's payable: commissions past their {facts.affiliateHoldDays}-day wait that haven't been cancelled or paid.
- We can only pay you once you've added a PayPal email. Make sure it's right: we send the money to the address you give us.
- Your payout history, with each PayPal transaction ID, is in **Settings** → **Affiliate program**.

## Taxes

You're responsible for reporting and paying any taxes on what you earn from the program.

## Promoting QRdx

- No spam: don't send unsolicited messages to promote QRdx, by email or anywhere else.
- Don't bid on "QRdx" or any spelling of it in search ads.
- Don't make misleading claims about QRdx, its features, its prices or the program, and don't present yourself as QRdx.
- Follow the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use-policy) wherever you share your link.

## Suspension and leaving

- We can suspend or close an affiliate account, for example if it breaks these terms. While an affiliate account is suspended, new payments from your referrals earn no commission and we send no payouts.
- You can leave the program at any time by emailing [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev).
- Deleting your QRdx account deletes your affiliate account with it, including its referrals and any commissions that haven't been paid.

## Changes

If we change these terms, we update this page and the date at the top.
