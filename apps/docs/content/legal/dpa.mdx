## 1. Scope and how it applies

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is part of the agreement between you ("Customer") and **[[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]]** ("QRdx") under the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service). It applies whenever QRdx processes Customer Personal Data while providing the Service. It takes effect when you accept the Terms and lasts as long as QRdx processes Customer Personal Data. If you need a signed copy for your records, email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev).

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms, this DPA wins for matters of personal data. If it conflicts with the Standard Contractual Clauses, the Clauses win.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Data Protection Law"** means every data protection law that applies to the processing, including the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA ("CCPA").
- **"Customer Personal Data"** means personal data that QRdx processes for Customer as part of the Service, mainly End User data.
- **"End Users"** means the people who scan Customer's codes, open Customer's hosted pages or fill in Customer's forms.
- **"Subprocessor"** means a third party QRdx engages to process Customer Personal Data.
- **"SCCs"** means the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission in Decision (EU) 2021/914.
- **"Security Incident"** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data.
- "Controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data" and "processing" have the meanings given in the GDPR. "Data Fiduciary" and "Data Processor" have the meanings given in the DPDP Act.

## 3. Roles

- Customer is the controller (a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act), or a processor acting for its own client. QRdx is the processor (a Data Processor), or a subprocessor.
- QRdx is a separate controller for its own account, billing, security and abuse-prevention data, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy). This DPA doesn't cover that data.

## 5. QRdx's obligations

QRdx will:

1. **Follow instructions.** Process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, which are the Terms, this DPA and Customer's use and settings of the Service, unless the law requires otherwise. If the law requires otherwise, QRdx will tell Customer first unless the law forbids it. QRdx will tell Customer if it believes an instruction breaks Data Protection Law.
2. **Keep it confidential.** Make sure everyone authorised to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality.
3. **Keep it secure.** Apply the technical and organisational measures in [Annex 2](#annex-2-security-measures).
4. **Use approved subprocessors only.** See section 6.
5. **Help with data subject requests.** Give Customer tools to find, export and delete End User data in the dashboard and the API. If QRdx receives a request directly from an End User, it will pass it to Customer where it can identify Customer and won't answer it itself unless Customer asks.
6. **Help with compliance.** Give Customer reasonable help, taking into account the information available to QRdx, with security, Security Incident notifications, data protection impact assessments and consultations with authorities.
7. **Report Security Incidents.** Notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data, with the information Customer needs to meet its own obligations, and keep Customer updated as more becomes known.
8. **Delete data at the end.** See section 11.
9. **Show compliance.** Make available the information needed to show compliance with this DPA, and allow audits as set out in section 10.

## 9. California (CCPA)

For personal information covered by the CCPA, QRdx is Customer's service provider. QRdx will not:

- sell or share the personal information;
- keep, use or disclose it for any purpose other than providing the Service, or outside the direct business relationship with Customer;
- combine it with personal information it receives from other sources, except as the CCPA allows.

QRdx will comply with the CCPA's obligations for service providers and tell Customer if it can no longer meet them. Customer may take reasonable steps to stop and fix unauthorised use.

## 10. Audits

QRdx will answer Customer's reasonable written questions about its data protection and security practices. If those answers aren't enough to show compliance, or a supervisory authority requires it, Customer may audit QRdx once a year, at Customer's cost, with at least 30 days' notice, during business hours, without disrupting the Service and under confidentiality. Audits don't cover other customers' data or the internal systems of QRdx's subprocessors, which QRdx can cover with the subprocessors' own reports where available.

## 11. Deletion at the end

Customer can export and delete Customer Personal Data at any time with the tools in the Service: deleting codes, pages, form responses and files, and exporting scans and responses as CSV. When Customer deletes its account, QRdx deletes the Customer Personal Data in that account's personal workspace. Data in a team workspace stays with the team for its remaining members. Deleted data can remain in the database provider's short point-in-time recovery history until it rolls over. QRdx may keep data the law requires it to keep, protected under this DPA, for as long as the law requires.

## Annex 2: Security measures

QRdx applies these measures to Customer Personal Data:

- **Encryption in transit.** Every connection to the Service, and between QRdx and its subprocessors, uses TLS.
- **Data minimisation.** Scanner IP addresses are never stored. Unique visitors are counted with a salted hash of IP address, user agent and date that changes every day. Only the host of a referring site is kept, not the full address. Rate-limit counters that may contain an IP address expire within 2 days.
- **Access control.** Customers sign in through Google or GitHub, and QRdx stores no passwords. Team access follows owner, admin and member roles. Only staff accounts can open the admin tools, and every staff action there is recorded in an audit log.
- **Credentials.** OAuth and API access tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes. Integration tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Passwords on protected codes are stored as hashes. Webhook payloads are signed with a per-endpoint secret.
- **Abuse and bot protection.** Rate limits, bot filtering, honeypot fields and Cloudflare Turnstile protect forms and public endpoints.
- **Infrastructure.** The Service runs on managed providers (Vercel and Neon) that maintain their own security certifications, with backups and point-in-time recovery for the database.
- **Isolation of hosted pages.** QRdx's own analytics and feedback tools don't load on Customer's hosted pages. Third-party pixels load only when Customer adds them, and wait for consent where the law requires.
- **Vulnerability handling.** Vulnerabilities can be reported under the [Security](/legal/security) policy, and fixes are made to the hosted Service and the `main` branch.
