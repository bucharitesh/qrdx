## How consent works

On your first visit to qrdx.dev we ask which optional categories you allow. Nothing optional loads until you choose, and **Reject all** is as easy as **Accept all**.

| Category | What it does | Tools | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Necessary** | Keeps you signed in, blocks form spam and remembers settings such as your theme and your cookie choice | QRdx, Cloudflare Turnstile | Always on |
| **Functionality** | Shows the feedback and support widget | Featurebase | Off until you allow it |
| **Analytics** | Measures which pages and features get used, so we know what to improve | PostHog | Off until you allow it |

- Your choice is saved in your browser in a first-party `c15t` cookie and localStorage entry for up to 12 months. We don't keep a copy on our servers, so you'll be asked again on a new browser or device.
- If you turn a category off, the page reloads without that tool, and the PostHog cookies and storage are deleted.

## Cookies

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lifetime | Category |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `better-auth.session_token` (named `__Secure-better-auth.session_token` over HTTPS) | QRdx | Keeps you signed in | 7 days, renewed while you use QRdx | Necessary |
| `better-auth.state` | QRdx | Protects signing in with Google or GitHub against forged requests | 5 minutes | Necessary |
| `better-auth.last_used_login_method` | QRdx | Remembers whether you last signed in with Google or GitHub | 30 days | Necessary |
| `<integration>_oauth_state` and `<integration>_code_verifier` | QRdx | Secure the step where you connect an integration such as Dub or Google Drive | 10 minutes | Necessary |
| `qrdx_currency` | QRdx | Remembers the currency you chose on the pricing page | 1 year | Necessary |
| `c15t` | QRdx | Remembers your cookie choices | Up to 12 months | Necessary |
| `ph_<project key>_posthog` | PostHog | Recognises your browser across visits for analytics | 1 year | Analytics |
| Featurebase cookies | Featurebase | Keep the feedback widget's conversation going | Set by Featurebase | Functionality |

Cloudflare Turnstile runs in a Cloudflare frame on the abuse report form and on hosted-page forms. Cloudflare may use its own storage there to tell people from bots, as described in its [Turnstile privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/).

## Browser storage

These items stay in your browser (localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB). They aren't sent to our servers unless the table says so, and they last until you clear them.

| Item | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `theme` | Light or dark mode |
| `editor-storage` | The design and settings in the QR editor, so your work is there when you come back |
| `preferences-storage` | Your download options and whether chat suggestions are open |
| `auth-storage` | An action to finish after you sign in, such as saving a code |
| `qrdx:recent-qr-types` | The QR types you used recently |
| `qrdx:ai-recent-themes` | AI themes you generated recently |
| `ai-chat-storage` (IndexedDB) | Your AI theme chat history. It goes to Google only when you send a new message in that chat. |
| `ai-local-draft-store` (IndexedDB) | An AI prompt you haven't sent yet, with its images |
| `qrdx:page-draft:<page>` | Unsaved changes to a hosted page you're editing |
| `qrdx-scanner` (IndexedDB) and `qrdx-scan-history` | The QR scanner's history (up to 500 entries) and whether it's on |
| `qrdx:mcp-client` | Which MCP client you picked on the MCP page |
| `qrdx:sound` | Whether interface sounds are muted. Sounds are on by default. |
| `qrdx:halloween-2026:hidden` | That you closed the seasonal banner |
| `qrdx:season-override` (sessionStorage) | A seasonal theme preview, for this tab only |
| `qrdx:default-theme-applied:<workspace>` (sessionStorage) | That your default theme was already applied in this tab |
| `ph_*` items | PostHog's analytics storage, only if you allow Analytics |

## On codes and hosted pages

QRdx's own banner, analytics and feedback widget never load on the pages your codes open. Visitors there may get these, set on behalf of the code's owner:

| Item | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `qrdx_unlock_<code>` (cookie) | Remembers that the visitor entered the right password for a protected code | 6 hours |
| `qrdx_consent:<page>` (localStorage) | The visitor's choice about the owner's tracking pixels | Until cleared |
| `qrdx:coupon:<page>:<coupon>` (localStorage) | Remembers that a coupon was already redeemed on this device | Until cleared |

Page views and clicks on hosted pages are counted with a cookieless beacon. If the owner adds tracking pixels (Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta, TikTok or LinkedIn), those set their own cookies. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and wherever a visitor's country is unknown, they wait for the visitor's consent; elsewhere they respect Global Privacy Control. Maps and videos the owner embeds are loaded from their providers.

Short links that only redirect set no cookies unless the code is password protected, and the `/m/` Easter-egg links set none and track nothing.

## Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you block necessary cookies, you won't be able to sign in.
