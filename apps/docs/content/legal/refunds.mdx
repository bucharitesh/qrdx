## Cancelling

1. Open **Manage subscription** in your settings. It takes you to the Polar billing portal.
2. Cancel the subscription there. You can also change plan, switch between monthly and yearly, and update your card.

After you cancel:

- Your paid plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. We don't charge you again.
- Then your account moves to Free. Every dynamic code keeps redirecting and every scan is still recorded. Analytics show the history the Free plan includes.
- If you're over a Free limit, you can't create more until you're back under it, but you can still change where existing codes point, archive them and export them.

Deleting your QRdx account also asks Polar to delete your customer record. Cancel any subscription first.

## Money-back guarantee

If a paid plan isn't right for you, you can have your first payment back.

- **What it covers.** The first payment for Pro or Business, billed monthly or yearly, including any tax charged on it.
- **How to ask.** Email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev) from the email address on your QRdx account within {facts.moneyBackDays} days of the payment, counted from the date on your receipt. You don't need to give a reason. A request sent in time counts even if we reply after the {facts.moneyBackDays} days are over.
- **Once per customer.** The guarantee applies to one payment per person or organization, also if you have several accounts or workspaces.
- **What it doesn't cover.** Renewals and any later payment, including the amount charged when you move to a bigger plan or from monthly to yearly. Those are looked at case by case, below.
- **What happens next.** We refund the payment in full through Polar to your original payment method and end the subscription at the same time, so you aren't charged again. Your account moves to Free straight away, and every dynamic code keeps redirecting. How long the money takes to reach you depends on your bank or card provider.

The guarantee doesn't apply to an account we closed for breaking our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use-policy).

## Other refunds

Subscriptions are paid in advance for each month or year. Outside the money-back guarantee, we look at refund requests case by case, for example if you were charged after forgetting to cancel, were charged twice, or couldn't use the plan because of a problem on our side.

To ask, email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev) from the email address on your account with the date of the charge and the reason. If we agree, the refund is issued through Polar to your original payment method.

AI art credits aren't money and can't be refunded, but credits for failed generations are returned to your monthly allowance automatically.

## Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any right your local consumer law gives you. If you're a consumer in the EEA or the UK, you may have a right to cancel within 14 days of buying, which can end once you start using the paid features with your agreement. Indian consumers keep their rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

## Merchant of record

[Polar](https://polar.sh) sells QRdx subscriptions as merchant of record. It processes payments, charges sales tax or VAT, issues invoices and handles chargebacks under its own [terms](https://polar.sh/legal/terms). If you have a billing problem, please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank, so we can sort it out quickly.
