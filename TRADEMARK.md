# QRdx trademark policy

The QRdx code is open source. The QRdx name, logo and the qrdx.dev domain are
not: they identify the official product, so people can tell what comes from us.
This policy says how you can and can't use them. It doesn't change the
licences of the code.

## You may, without asking

- Say your product works with, integrates with or is built on QRdx, as long as
  it's true and doesn't suggest we made, endorse or support it.
- Use the name "QRdx" in articles, tutorials, talks, reviews and comparisons.
- Link to qrdx.dev and show the unmodified logo next to that link.
- Run QRdx for yourself or your organisation (self-hosting), keeping the name
  internally.

## You may not

- Offer a fork, a hosted service or a product under the QRdx name or logo, or a
  name or logo confusingly similar to them (for example "QRdx Cloud" or
  "QRdx Pro" for something we don't run).
- Use "QRdx" in a domain, company name, app store listing or social handle for
  something that isn't ours.
- Change the logo, combine it with your own mark, or use it in a way that
  suggests a partnership we haven't agreed to.

## Forks and self-hosted public services

If you run a modified version or a public service built from this code, give it
your own name and logo and remove ours from the interface, emails and metadata.
The AGPL-3.0 (and other) licence notices must stay.

## Questions

Email [support@qrdx.dev](mailto:support@qrdx.dev) if you're unsure or want
written permission. We can change this policy over time; the version in this
repository is current.
